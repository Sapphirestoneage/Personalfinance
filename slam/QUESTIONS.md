## Phase 2, Batch G: what the Budget calendar does that SLAM does not yet (2026-10-23)

The Budget calendar is not retired yet: a second audit of its own code found these (S-209, `docs/CALENDAR_PARITY.md`, "The gap audit"). The retirement is ready as `docs/phase2/batch-g-retire.patch`.

29. **Paid from a second account**: in the Budget a bill can come out of savings or a second checking account, and a card or loan payment out of a chosen account. SLAM's day-by-day engine (the vendored v44 copy, never edited, S-203) takes every bill and payment from main checking. Paychecks already land in their own account. Allow a small change to the engine (a "from" account on each payment), or keep every bill and payment on main checking?
30. **Card and loan payments as items**: in the Budget a card or loan payment can be moved, skipped, marked "It happened" or matched to a log entry. In SLAM the engine pays them itself on the due day by the card's pay setting, so none of that applies to them (paychecks and bills have all of it). Allow the engine change, or keep payments automatic?
31. **The cushion**: the Budget had a typed "keep at least this much in checking". SLAM's floor is the stage 1 month less what the cushion accounts hold (your correction in Phase 1). Keep the derived floor only, or add a typed override?
32. **A forecast saved on the 1st**: the Budget saved each month's predicted month-end checking when the month opened and later compared it with the real end. SLAM compares the forecast with the real balance at every check-in (drift). Is drift enough, or save a month-start forecast too (a new stored field)?
33. **Balance checks per account**: in the Budget a balance typed for one account, or one debt, on any day reset that account from that day while the others kept projecting. A SLAM check-in moves the whole household to that day. Keep the check-in as is, or add per-account dated checks (and debts)?
34. **Two named semimonthly pay days**: the Budget stores both days (say the 1st and the 15th). SLAM stores one, and the engine's rule makes the second: a day before the 15th gives it and 15 days later (the 1st gives the 1st and 16th), the 16th to the 27th gives it and 15 days earlier, and the 15th or the 28th and later give the 15th and the last day. Add a second pay-day field so the 1st and 15th work?
35. **A debt paid by a budget line**: in the Budget a debt can be linked to the line that pays it, so it is scheduled once. In SLAM a car payment entered as an expense and the car loan's minimum would both go out. Add the link?
36. **Everyday spending across the month**: SLAM spreads each line evenly over the month and moves cash only when what you log goes past the plan; the Budget spread what was left of the plan (the plan less what was logged) over the days left. Month-end totals agree; the day-to-day shape differs. Keep SLAM's way?
37. **A category planned as one number**: the Budget could plan a whole category as one amount and spread it. SLAM plans line by line. Needed?
38. **A card paid in full every month**: under the v44 rule a card with a balance pays interest at its first statement even when it is set to pay the statement in full (it may have been carried). Should a card set to "pays the statement" be treated as never carrying a balance?
39. **Retire now or later**: with 29 to 37 answered either way, the patch can go in. Retire now and treat what is left as known differences, or wait until the ones you want are built?
