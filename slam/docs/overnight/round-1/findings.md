# Round 1 findings (fresh-eyes review)

Judged only from docs/REVIEW_KIT.md, docs/FEATURES.md, the three reports and the 46 screenshots in this folder.

## Verdict

SLAM looks like real software. The type, the dark mode, the bordered buttons, the one-line rows with chevrons, the "Show as table" twins and the hatched marks for rough inputs are consistent and calm. Today, My numbers, Profile and Got extra money? pass Krug's trunk test. The problem is trust. The household's numbers are worked out by several engines that do not agree, and an ex-auditor or a CPA will see it within a minute. There are three card balances per card, three FI years, three savings rates, two take-home figures and two debt-free dates. A cash forecast says checking goes to -$227 while the paycheck plan says every dollar is covered. agree-check passes because it only watches 21 tagged metrics: none of the numbers that disagree here are tagged, and it reads 15% and 15.4% as the same. qa-crawl also passes even though the phone Debt screen renders 670px wide. Until those engines share one source, the polish works against the app, because it makes wrong numbers look certain. Fix the "Numbers that disagree" section first.
