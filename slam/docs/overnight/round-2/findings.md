# Round 2 findings

Overall verdict: SLAM looks like real software now. The shell is calm and consistent at both sizes, the charts mostly follow the standard (question titles, Show as table, dashed or hatched guesses), and the agree-check passes for the 21 registered metrics. The trust problems are in numbers the agree-check does not register. The Plan's monthly amount reads $712 in one place and $495, $478 or $3,896 per paycheck in another. The Plan's low-interest debt is $1,200 too high. The Debt screen's interest column adds to more than its own total. The Session Needs lines do not add to the Needs total. Two projections of the same future disagree by about $300k. The Calendar also tells the client to send $712 to debt on Oct 31 while its own cash line leaves that payment out. Eli and Tom would catch every one of these on a live call. Below the numbers, the main noise is the same "Move $228" message on four screens, a Debt screen nearly 7,000px tall at 390px, and a few charts whose colors or dots mean different things. These screens are fine apart from what is listed: Income, Profile, Net worth, Where the money goes, and Got extra money? (whose open issue is Q158).
