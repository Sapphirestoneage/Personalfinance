# Round 3 findings

The app now looks like real software. The tiles, rows, buttons and charts follow one system in light and dark, the core figures on Today, My numbers and the One-pager agree (agree-check passes on 21 metrics), and the screens without debt or plan logic are calm and fine. Trust breaks on the derived numbers, and agree-check does not track any of them. The Debt screen tells three different stories about the same two cards: the Visa is tagged "Paid last" but listed second. Its "Interest, next 12 months" column adds up to more than the lifetime interest. The Store card's $360 deferred-interest warning never shows in the month-by-month interest. The Paycheck plan sends $495 and $478 "to the Plan" while the Plan says $712 a month. The savings rate says "of take-home" right beside a take-home figure that gives a different rate. The Needs lines do not add up to their own total. The Plan dates a goal that the Goals screen says has no pace. Eli (an ex-auditor) and Tom (a CPA) would catch each of these within seconds. Lower down, the main noise is the same "Move $228" banner on four tabs, folds and forms open that nobody asked for, and the Debt screen running to 6,950px on a phone. qa-crawl passes but still flags 399 tap targets under 44px.
