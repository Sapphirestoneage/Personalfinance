# Formulas

Every metric the app shows, from the one registry (`engine/math.js`). Show the math, the One-pager and every screen
read the same entry. Written by `node tools/formulas-doc.mjs`; do not edit by hand. The hand-checked tests are in
`test/p25-b-formulas.test.js` (the Phase 2.5 definitions) and the golden tests (`test/demo.golden.test.js` and others).

Families of metrics built per item (one card, one account, one Plan step, one DRAFTT letter) follow the same
pattern and are listed in the registry under `cardUtil:`, `maxit:`, `plan:` and the rest.

| Metric | Formula | In plain words | Reads | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Total assets (`assets`) | Total assets = every account and asset balance added up | Every account and everything you own, added up. | Numbers | dollars |
| Total debts (`debts`) | Total debts = every debt balance added up | Everything you owe, added up. | Numbers | dollars |
| Net worth (`netWorth`) | Total net worth = all assets + owed to you - all debt | Everything you own, the house and car included, and what people owe you, less everything you owe. | Numbers | dollars |
| Liquid assets (`liquidAssets`) | Liquid assets = cash, taxable investing and other accounts counted as liquid (a Roth IRA counts its contributions only) | Money you could reach without a penalty or a sale: cash, a brokerage, and what you put into a Roth IRA. | Numbers, Profile | dollars |
| Reachable after all debts (liquid net worth) (`liquidNetWorth`) | Liquid net worth = liquid assets (cash, taxable, Roth contributions) - all debt | What you could reach without a penalty or selling something you live with, less everything you owe. | Numbers, Profile | dollars |
| Cash (`cash`) | Cash = checking, savings and other cash accounts added up (the Roth backstop and money set aside for a goal are not cash) | Checking, savings and other cash, less money set aside for a goal. | Numbers, Intentions | dollars |
| Invested assets (`invested`) | Invested = retirement, IRA, HSA and taxable investing balances added up | Retirement accounts, IRAs, an HSA and a brokerage, added up. | Numbers | dollars |
| Gross income this month (`gross`) | Gross income = each income stream's amount for this month (certain income only) | Pay before any tax or saving comes out, this month. | Numbers | dollars a month |
| Take-home this month (`takeHome`) | Take-home = gross income - tax withheld - Social Security and Medicare - payroll saving | Pay after tax and what your paycheck saves for you, this month. | Numbers, Rules | dollars a month |
| Spending this month (`expenses`) | Spending = each expense this month (your share of a shared bill; $0 when someone else covers it) | What you spend this month: your share of a shared bill, and $0 for a bill someone else covers. | Numbers, Profile | dollars a month |
| Subscriptions a month (`subscriptions`) | Subscriptions = each subscription's cost to you a month | What your subscriptions cost you a month. | Numbers | dollars a month |
| Lean spending this month (`leanSpending`) | Lean spending = food, housing and transportation that are needs (a want such as eating out is left out) | Food, housing and getting around that are needs. | Numbers, Profile | dollars a month |
| Minimum payments this month (`minimums`) | Minimum payments = each debt's minimum this month (a rough default when none is entered) | The least each debt asks for this month. | Numbers, House defaults | dollars a month |
| Tax set-aside this month (`taxSetAside`) | Tax set-aside = the year's tax still due (tax - withholding - estimated payments - already set aside) / months until the return is due; while the tax cannot be figured, untaxed income x the marginal rate | What to put aside each month for tax not taken out of pay. | Numbers, Rules | dollars a month |
| Monthly surplus (`surplus`) | Monthly surplus = take-home - spending - debt minimums - tax set-aside on 1099 income | What is free each month to save or pay down debt, before any saving: take-home pay less spending, the minimum on every debt and the tax set aside on pay with no tax taken out. | Numbers, Rules, House defaults | dollars a month |
| Left after saving (`leftAfterSaving`) | Left after saving = monthly surplus - saving from take-home into investment accounts | The monthly surplus less what already goes from take-home into a Roth, a brokerage or another investment account. Saving into a cash account stays in cash, so it is not taken off. | Numbers, Rules, House defaults | dollars a month |
| Savings rate (`savingsRateNet`) | Savings rate = (savings + pre-tax retirement + employer match + extra debt payoff above minimums) / (take-home + pre-tax retirement + employer match) | The share of your pay that is set to be saved. Saving counts what goes to savings and investments, to retirement from your paycheck, the match your employer adds, and any extra paid on debt above the minimums. Money left over but not assigned is not counted. It is measured against take-home pay, with the pre-tax saving and the match added back. | Numbers, Rules, House defaults, Intentions | a share (percent) |
| Savings rate of gross (`savingsRateGross`) | Savings rate of gross = (savings + pre-tax retirement + employer match + extra debt payoff above minimums) / (gross income + employer match) | The same saving measured against gross pay (before any tax) plus the employer match. Shown beside the headline in Show the math. | Numbers, Rules, House defaults, Intentions | a share (percent) |
| Cash runway (`runway`) | Cash runway = (liquid cash - money set aside for goals) / (monthly spending + debt minimums) | How many months your cash lasts at today's spending if no pay came in. Money set aside for a goal is not counted as cash. | Numbers, Intentions, House defaults | months |
| Runway on food, housing and transportation (`runwayFat`) | FAT runway = cash / (food, housing and transportation spending + minimum payments) | How many months cash lasts paying only food, housing, getting around and debt minimums. | Numbers, House defaults | months |
| Cash runway on needs only (`runwayNeeds`) | Runway on needs = cash (not money set aside for goals) / (spending marked need + debt minimums) | How many months the cash lasts if only the needs are paid: the lines marked need and the minimum on every debt. | Numbers, Profile, House defaults | months |
| Cushion step 1: a month of needs (`cushionNeeds`) | Step 1 = a lean month of needs = spending marked need + debt minimums | The first step of the cushion: enough cash for one month of the needs alone. | Numbers, Profile, House defaults | dollars a month |
| Cushion step 2: a month of full spending (`cushionMonth`) | Step 2 = a full month of spending = spending + debt minimums | The second step: enough cash for one whole month as you live now. | Numbers, House defaults | dollars a month |
| Cushion target (months) (`ruleOf5Months`) | Months to keep = age / 5 | The cushion target: one month of full spending for every five years of age. | Profile, House defaults | months |
| Cushion step 3: the full cushion (`ruleOf5Dollars`) | Step 3 = (age / 5) months x a month of full spending, rounded up to whole dollars | The third and last step of the cushion: the full target in dollars. | Numbers, Profile, House defaults | dollars |
| Credit card balances (`cards`) | Cards = every credit card balance added up | What is on every credit card. | Numbers | dollars |
| Promo balances due (`promoTotal`) | Promo balances = each balance still on a 0% intro rate | Balances still at a 0% intro rate. | Numbers | dollars |
| Monthly amount that clears the promos (`promoMonthly`) | Monthly to clear the promos = each promo balance / the payments left before its intro ends, added up | What a month clears every promo balance before its rate goes up. | Numbers | dollars a month |
| Personal card utilization (`personalUtil`) | Card use (personal) = balances / limits on the cards that report to personal credit | How much of your personal cards' limits is in use, the number credit scores read. | Numbers | a share (percent) |
| All-card utilization (`overallUtil`) | Card use (all cards) = balances / limits on every card, business cards included | The same across every card, business cards too. | Numbers | a share (percent) |
| Housing as a share of take-home (`housingShare`) | Housing reference = housing a month / take-home, beside 30% with a car or 35% without (a reference, not a target) | What housing takes out of take-home pay, shown beside Eli's reference line: 30% with a car, 35% without. It is a reference point, never a grade. | Numbers, Rules, House defaults | a share (percent) |
| FI number from today's spending (`fiToday`) | FI number (today) = today's yearly spending, debt payments included / withdrawal rate | What you would need invested to live as you do now, debt payments and all, from the withdrawal rate alone. | Numbers, Assumptions, House defaults | dollars |
| FI number without debt payments (`impliedFi`) | FI number (no debt) = (yearly spending - debt payments) / withdrawal rate | The same, once the debts are paid off: today's spending without the debt payments. | Numbers, Assumptions | dollars |
| FI number from your goal (`fiNumber`) | FI number (goal) = goal FI spending a year / withdrawal rate (or the FI number you set); with no goal, today's FI number | What you would need invested to live the way you plan to at FI. It is the number years to FI and Coast FI aim at. | Intentions, Assumptions, Numbers | dollars |
| FI percent (`fiPercent`) | FI percent = invested assets / FI number (today's) | How far along you are: what is invested now against what living as you do now would take. | Numbers, Assumptions, House defaults | a share (percent) |
| Lean FI (`leanFi`) | Lean FI = yearly needs (food, housing, transportation) / withdrawal rate | Enough invested to cover only food, housing and getting around, the needs, with nothing extra. | Numbers, Profile, Assumptions | dollars |
| Fat FI (`fatFi`) | Fat FI = dream yearly spending / withdrawal rate | Enough invested to live the life you would pick with no limits: your dream spending a year. | Intentions, Assumptions | dollars |
| Barista FI (`baristaFi`) | Barista FI = (yearly spending - part-time income a year) / withdrawal rate | Enough invested that part-time work covers the rest: today's yearly spending less what part-time pay brings in. | Numbers, Intentions, Assumptions, House defaults | dollars |
| Coast FI (`coastFi`) | Coast FI = FI number / (1 + real return) ^ (years to the target age) | Enough invested now that, with nothing more added, growth alone reaches the FI number by the target age. | Intentions, Assumptions, Profile, Rules | dollars |
| Years to FI (`yearsToFi`) | Years to FI = months, run month by month (income starting and ending, debts paid off, tax set aside, each month's surplus invested), until invested money reaches the FI number | How many years until what is invested reaches the FI number, month by month. | Numbers, Intentions, Assumptions, Rules | years |
| Tax balance due (`taxBalanceDue`) | Balance due = federal and state tax for the year - tax withheld - estimated payments made | This year's tax not yet paid through withholding or estimates. | Numbers, Rules | dollars |
| Roth backstop (contributions) (`rothBasis`) | Roth backstop = Roth IRA contribution basis (comes out any time with no tax or penalty; never counted as cash) | What you put into a Roth IRA, which can come out with no tax or penalty. Never counted as cash. | Numbers, Rules | dollars |
| Costs once certain income stops (`cliffCosts`) | Costs = spending + minimum payments + tax set-aside in the first month certain income is $0 | What a month costs once pay you can count on stops. | Numbers, Rules, House defaults | dollars a month |

DRAFTT (`plan:draftt:<letter>`): DRAFTT share = the letter's amount a month / take-home (Retirement / gross). Shares only, no bands. Reads Numbers and House defaults (the housing reference).
