# Partially employed: Dev Okonkwo (persona 12, 31, made up)

File: `households/12-partially-employed.json`. Walkthrough: `12-partially-employed-walkthrough.md`.

## Who he is

Dev is 31 and lives in Buffalo with a roommate. He works 24 hours a week at
a bookstore ($19 an hour, $456 a week) and fills the year with seasonal work:
a summer festival this past June to August ($4,800 in total) and, every year,
tax preparation from mid-January to mid-April ($3,200 a month). There is a
possible full-time offer at a credit union from May 2027 that he puts at 40%.
He buys a bronze marketplace plan with a subsidy, has a Roth IRA from a
better year, a small Visa balance and a federal loan on an income-based plan.

His money question is not FIRE. It is smoothing: which months are short,
how much to hold back from the tax-season months for the summer, whether the
subsidy reconciles against him when his income jumps, and whether the offer
changes any of it.

## What he would want the app to tell him

- A month-by-month cash line for the next 12 months with the seasonal streams in it: which months dip, what to hold back in February and March.
- The subsidy reconciliation: at about $28,000 of income he gets a subsidy; a $4,300 a month job from May changes the year's income and the subsidy at tax time.
- His hourly reality: 24 hours at $19 is the number he thinks in; the app wants a weekly amount.
- Whether the income-based loan payment changes when the seasonal months are counted.
- What the offer would do to the picture at 40%, 100% and 0%, side by side.
