# Walkthrough: Grace and Daniel Park, Chubby FI

Method and shared findings: `UI-NOTES.md`.

## What the pages showed

- Ledger: loaded, 107 fields; two people; both salaries.
- Dashboard: loaded (Westchester). Net worth $3,707,000. Runway 10.6 months (green). Surplus $300 after $6,250 a month of contributions. Savings rate 21.8%. FI **74.1%** of $3,400,000; "FI in about 3.3 years" (Likely set; 3.5 at their 5%); Coast FI reached. 2026 refund $18,326. Only two flags, both "uses 1 rough number".
- The projection: the college line ends May 2029, the mortgage ends November 2036, no shortfall.

## No field for

- Health insurance from 55 to 65 at no subsidy (about $2,400 a month for two): an expense that starts in the future, file-only, and a number they cannot know yet.
- The net investment income tax (3.8% on $34,000 of investment income this year, far more after they stop working).
- A drawdown: which account first, Roth conversions in the low-income years, required minimum distributions from 73.
- Daniel's catch-up: "catch-up eligible: yes" is a stored fact nothing reads; the $2,708.33 contribution had to be typed with the catch-up already in it.
- A pension or Social Security in the FI number later.

## Forced into the wrong category

- The 529 is in the education family and rightly not FI money; the college line is "Everything else" with an end date in the file.
- Two cars as one "Car" item (to keep the list short) lose 15% a year by house default.

## Confusing labels or steps

- "FI goal covers today's spending" is green; the comparison uses spending that includes $2,000 a month of college that ends in 2029 and excludes the $3,100 mortgage that ends in 2036 and the health premiums that start at 55. The number is right for today and wrong for the plan.

## Results that look wrong or misleading

- The refund is overstated by the net investment income tax (about $1,300) and whatever the 2026 bonus and stock would add; fine for a household this size, but "every input known" is not the same as "every tax modeled".

## Assumptions that are not true for them

- The plan ends at FI. Everything after (drawing, converting, Medicare) is out of scope.

## Rating

**Works with workarounds** (the accumulation picture is right; the questions that matter to them start where the app stops).
