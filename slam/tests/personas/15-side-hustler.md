# Persona 15: Casey Rivera, 32, side hustler (made up)

Reuses the app's own demo household, `../../fixtures/demo.json` (hand math in
`../../test/demo.golden.test.js`). Walkthrough: `15-side-hustler-walkthrough.md`.

## Who they are

Casey is 32, a salaried employee at a health system in Albany ($72,000, W-2,
with a 401(k) and a 100%-of-the-first-4% match) who tutors on weekends for
about $500 a month, paid by families on 1099s with nothing withheld. There is
a Roth IRA, a brokerage account, a car, two cards (one on a 0% store promo
that ends in February 2027, with deferred interest), and a federal student
loan. A possible spring bonus is 50% likely.

The side hustle is the point: a second income that carries its own tax, its
own quarterly dates, and a question about whether it is worth the Saturday.

## Every number they would enter

The demo file holds them. In the app's words: a paycheck (W-2) monthly with
withholding; freelance or contract (1099) monthly, rough, no withholding;
maybe money once in March 2027 at 50%; rent, utilities, groceries, eating
out, bus pass and rideshare, phone, streaming, gym, everything else; a
checking account and a high-yield savings account; a 401(k) with a monthly
contribution and the match as text; a Roth IRA with contributions by year
and a basis; a brokerage account; a car losing 12% a year; two cards and a
business card; a federal loan on the standard plan; an FI goal of $40,000 a
year of spending and a six-month cushion goal.

## What they would want the app to tell them

- The quarterly estimate: not just that January 15 is the next date, but how much to send, and whether the W-2 withholding already covers the tutoring (safe harbor).
- The tutoring's real after-tax hourly rate: $500 less 22% federal, 5.4% state and 14.1% self-employment tax is about $293.
- Whether to raise W-2 withholding instead of paying estimates (one form, no dates).
- The promo deadline on the store card against the surplus, which the app does well.
- What the tutoring does to the FI date, and what dropping it does.
