# Persona 1: Mila Soto, 24, sugar baby (made up)

File: `households/01-sugar-baby.json`. Walkthrough: `01-sugar-baby-walkthrough.md`.

## Who she is

Mila is 24, lives alone in a studio in Manhattan, and works part time at a
clothing boutique. For most of this year she has been in an arrangement with
an older man she met through a dating site: he sends her a monthly allowance
by Venmo or in cash, usually around $3,000 but anywhere from $2,000 to $4,500,
and some months he pays her rent directly instead. He also gives her things:
a handbag, flights and hotels when she travels with him, dinners. She is not
naive about it; she calls it what it is, she keeps her own apartment and her
own job, and she knows it could end any month.

She buys her own health insurance on the marketplace. She has a small Roth
IRA she opened on a friend's advice, one credit card she is behind on, and a
buy now pay later balance from a phone. What she wants is to turn two
unpredictable years into something that lasts: an exit fund, a plan for the
tax question nobody will answer plainly, and a sense of whether she can keep
the apartment if the allowance stops.

## What she would want the app to tell her

- Is the allowance taxable? If it is a gift it is not her income (he may owe gift tax above $19,000 a year); if it is payment for companionship it is self-employment income with no withholding. She wants the number both ways and a set-aside she can trust.
- How many months could she cover without the allowance, and what would she have to cut first.
- Whether to pay down the Visa or build the exit fund first, with the 26.99% in front of her.
- A way to enter an income that is likely but not certain, and that varies, without the app treating it as either a sure $3,000 or nothing.
- Where the in-kind gifts go: she knows a $1,500 trip is not money she can save, but she would like the picture to show that her lifestyle costs more than her cash shows.
- Health insurance: the marketplace subsidy depends on income she cannot predict; what happens at reconciliation.
