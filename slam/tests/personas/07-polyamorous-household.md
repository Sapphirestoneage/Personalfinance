# Persona 7: Tess, Omar and Lin, a polyamorous household (made up)

File: `households/07-polyamorous-household.json` (Tess and Omar's view).
Walkthrough: `07-polyamorous-household-walkthrough.md`.

## Who they are

Tess (38) and Omar (41) are married and have lived with Lin (35) for four
years; the three of them are a family, one household in every sense but the
legal one. Tess directs programs at a nonprofit ($78,000, W-2). Omar does
contract UX work on 1099s, about $4,200 a month before $600 of expenses, with
a marketplace health plan and a SEP IRA. Lin is a nurse ($92,000) with her
own accounts, her own debts and her own plan; she is not in this file.

They split some costs three ways and others by agreement: rent, utilities,
internet and groceries are shared, with Tess and Omar paying two-thirds as a
couple and Lin one-third. Phones, transit, Omar's therapy and health plan,
and each person's debts are their own. They are saving, three ways, for a
down payment on a bigger place. Tess and Omar file jointly; Lin files single.

The question they bring to a coach is the one the app has no shape for:
three adults, two tax returns, one home, costs shared by a formula that is
not an even split and not one person's share of a two-person bill.

## What they would want the app to tell them

- A view of the whole house (three incomes, the shared bills once) and a view per tax return, without typing the shared bills twice or pretending Lin is a roommate who covers things.
- Their two-thirds share done right, and a way to change the formula (by income, not by head) without re-entering four bills.
- Omar's quarterly taxes on the 1099 income, after the SEP deduction and the self-employed health insurance deduction.
- What the down payment looks like three ways, and whose name goes on what.
- Runway for the couple when Lin's third is a certainty and Omar's contract income is not.
