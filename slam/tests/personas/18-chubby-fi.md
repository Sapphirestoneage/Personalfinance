# Persona 18: Grace and Daniel Park, 49 and 50, Chubby FI (made up)

File: `households/18-chubby-fi.json`. Walkthrough: `18-chubby-fi-walkthrough.md`.

## Who they are

Grace (49) is a director at a pharmaceutical company ($228,000); Daniel (50)
is an architect ($132,000). They live in Westchester in a house worth
$1,250,000 with a $320,000 mortgage at 3.1%, have one son in college (two
more years, $2,000 a month after the 529), and spend about $10,600 a month
plus the mortgage. They have $2,520,000 invested and want to stop at 55 with
a comfortable life, not a frugal one: a "chubby" number of $3,400,000.

Their questions are the ones that appear above $2 million: health insurance
before 65 at a high income (no subsidy), Roth conversions in the early
retirement years, the order to draw from taxable, pre-tax and Roth, whether
to pay off a 3.1% mortgage, and sequence-of-returns risk in the first years.

## What they would want the app to tell them

- The FI date at $3,400,000 (the app says about 3.5 years), and what the mortgage ending in 2036 does to spending after.
- Marketplace premiums from 55 to 65 at zero subsidy: about $2,400 a month for two, a line they cannot enter as starting in the future except in the file.
- A drawdown order and the tax on it: the app has no decumulation, no Roth conversion planning, no required minimum distributions.
- The 3.8% net investment income tax on their $34,000 of investment income (not modeled) and the 0.9% additional Medicare tax (modeled).
- Whether the number is "chubby" enough: $3,400,000 at 4% is $136,000 a year; their spending plus taxes and health insurance after 55 might be $150,000.
