# Persona 19: Victor Lam, 54, Fat FI (made up)

File: `households/19-fat-fi.json`. Walkthrough: `19-fat-fi-walkthrough.md`.

## Who he is

Victor is 54, single, a managing director at a bank in Manhattan: $420,000 in
salary, $160,000 of RSUs vesting each quarter, and a bonus around $250,000
each February. He has $6,000,000 invested, a co-op worth $3,200,000 with a
$900,000 mortgage, and spends about $22,000 a month plus the mortgage. His
number is $8,000,000 and he expects to reach it at 56 or 57.

His questions are the ones a fat-FI plan raises: the 3.8% net investment
income tax and the 20% gains band, the additional Medicare tax, New York City
tax, estate planning, what to do with the co-op, how to keep the plan from
depending on the bonus, and whether a 4% rate is right for a 40-year horizon.

## What he would want the app to tell him

- A tax estimate that includes the net investment income tax ($7,220 on $190,000 of investment income), New York City tax (about $25,000), the 20% gains band (modeled) and the additional Medicare tax (modeled).
- The FI date with and without the bonus, and with the mortgage paid off from cash (he could).
- His real monthly surplus. The app shows minus $21,100 because the Amex he pays in full is counted as a $14,000 debt payment, and the payoff orders say the Amex "never pays off" with two billion dollars of interest.
- Withdrawal tax planning at this size: a 40-year horizon, the 20% band, the Roth question, charity from appreciated shares.
- What the co-op and mortgage do to a "fat" number that is supposed to be liquid.
