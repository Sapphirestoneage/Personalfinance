# Slow FIRE: Maya Lindgren (persona 16, 33, made up)

File: `households/16-slow-fi.json`. Walkthrough: `16-slow-fi-walkthrough.md`.

## Who she is

Maya is 33, a UX researcher in Brooklyn ($110,400), and has decided against the
race. She saves about 20% of her pay (a 401(k) with a match, a Roth IRA, a
brokerage account), spends on travel and climbing and therapy without guilt,
and wants financial independence at 55, not 45. She is planning a six-month
sabbatical in 2028 (March to August), funded from a $28,000 account she has
built for it, and expects to return to the same kind of work after.

The questions she brings are about pace, not speed: is 20% enough for 55,
what the sabbatical costs in years, and whether she can let travel grow with
her salary.

## What she would want the app to tell her

- Am I on track for 55, yes or no, and by how much. The app gives "FI in about 21 years" (2047) with no reference to her date.
- What the sabbatical costs: six months with no pay and no contributions, and whether the fund covers it (it does: the projection shows no shortfall).
- Whether 20% is the right rate for her date, and what each extra 1% buys in months.
- Permission to spend: a savings rate target she sets, with the rest of the surplus marked as hers to use.
- Coast FI at 55, not 65: the app's coast age is a single number she can change, and she would.
