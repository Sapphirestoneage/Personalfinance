# Persona 13: Ana Reyes, 45, Lean FI (made up)

File: `households/13-lean-fi.json`. Walkthrough: `13-lean-fi-walkthrough.md`.

## Who she is

Ana is 45, a lab technician in Rochester ($55,200), and has lived on about
$2,100 a month for fifteen years by choice: a small apartment, a bike, the bus,
groceries, a few subscriptions, a hundred dollars a month to causes she cares
about. Her essentials, food, housing and transportation, come to $1,660 a
month. She has $480,000 invested across a 401(k), a Roth IRA and a brokerage
account and keeps $25,000 in cash.

Her definition of done is Lean FI: enough to cover food, a roof and getting
around, forever, at a 4% withdrawal rate. That is $498,000. She is at 96% of
it and plans to keep working one more year, then take a job she likes better
for less, or none. She does not want the app to tell her she needs $631,500
because that is what all her spending implies; the extra is optional and
she knows it.

## What she would want the app to tell her

- Her Lean FI number by her definition ($1,660 x 12 / 4% = $498,000) and her percent of it (96%), as the headline, not a footnote under a bigger number.
- Not to be flagged red for having a goal "below what today's spending implies": the gap is the point.
- The date she crosses it at the current pace (months, not years), and what one more year of saving adds.
- What happens to health insurance when the salary stops (the marketplace at $20,000 of income).
- A Barista or part-time option: how much income closes the gap between lean and full spending.
