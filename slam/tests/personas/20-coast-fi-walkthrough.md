# Walkthrough, Coast FIRE: Sofia Bianchi (persona 20)

Method and shared findings: `UI-NOTES.md`.

## What the pages showed

- Ledger: loaded, 70 fields.
- Dashboard: loaded. Net worth $343,000. Runway 7.7 months (green; the Rule of 5 target is 7.2, $20,484, met). Surplus $207. Savings rate 5.3%. FI **31.0%** of $1,000,000; "Coast FI by 65: $184,557" (Likely set, 6%; $242,946 at her own 5%), reached either way; "FI in about 18.0 years" (21.1 at 5%). 2026 refund $1,234. Two flags, both "uses 1 rough number".

## No field for

- "Coast reached" as a state. The tile prints the coast number next to "invested $310,000" and leaves the comparison to her; a one-line "you are past Coast FI; growth alone gets you to $1,000,000 by 57" is the sentence she came for.
- The Worst set side by side: at 4% real and 3.5% withdrawal the coast number is $370,000 and the FI number $1,142,857, so she is not past it. The sets are a toggle, not a comparison.
- "What would $200 a month buy back": no contribution scenario.

## Forced into the wrong category

- Nothing.

## Confusing labels or steps

- The FI tile's "31.0%" headline reads as "far away" for someone whose plan is to be exactly here.
- The Dashboard's default Likely set (6%) is more optimistic than her own 5%; the coast number on first load is $60,000 lower than hers.

## Results that look wrong or misleading

- None in the numbers.

## Assumptions that are not true for her

- Progress is the percent of the FI number. For Coast FI, progress is "the growth is doing the work", which the app computes and does not say.

## Rating

**Works well** (with the Likely default and the lack of a worst-case comparison as the two things to know).
