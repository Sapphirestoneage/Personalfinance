# Persona 5: Priya Raman and Naomi Katz, 34 and 36, lesbian couple (made up)

File: `households/05-lesbian-couple.json`. Walkthrough: `05-lesbian-couple-walkthrough.md`.

## Who they are

Priya (34) is a hospital administrator; Naomi (36) teaches high school. They
married in 2021, rent in Westchester, and are starting a family by reciprocal
IVF in early 2027: Priya's eggs, Naomi carrying. New York's insurance mandate
makes Naomi's large-group plan cover part of it, so the clinic's cost to them
is about $18,000, plus $5,000 of medications and $3,600 for three vials of
donor sperm with shipping and storage. After the birth they will do a
second-parent adoption or a parentage order so that Priya is a legal parent
everywhere, about $3,500 in lawyer and court fees. They have been saving for
this in a dedicated account for two years.

They have two retirement plans, a car loan, a Visa they pay in full, and a
long-run FI goal they do not talk about much yet. Their questions are about
sequencing: whether the IVF fund is enough, whether to pause retirement
contributions for a year, what the medical expense deduction does, and how
the budget changes with a baby.

## What they would want the app to tell them

- Is the $32,000 enough for $30,100 of dated costs, and what the cash looks like in February and March 2027.
- Whether pausing the 403(b) for a year changes the FI date by much.
- The tax angle: IVF, medications and donor costs are medical expenses, deductible only above 7.5% of AGI ($11,430 at their income), so around $15,000 of the $26,600 might be deductible if they itemize; a dependent-care FSA next year; whether the adoption credit applies (it does not for adopting a spouse's child, and a parentage order is not an adoption).
- How a second parent's income and accounts show up as hers, not as a copy of the first person's.
- What the budget looks like with a baby: childcare, a new line they cannot size yet.
