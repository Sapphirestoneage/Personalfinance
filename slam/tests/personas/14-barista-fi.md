# Barista FIRE: Theo Marsh (persona 14, 52, made up)

File: `households/14-barista-fi.json`. Walkthrough: `14-barista-fi-walkthrough.md`.

## Who he is

Theo is 52 and left a software job at 50 with $690,000 invested. He works 20
hours a week at a coffee roaster in Albany, mostly for the health plan, for
$1,650 a month before tax. He spends about $3,200 a month and covers the gap
of about $1,750 by selling from his brokerage account, roughly $21,000 a year,
a 3% draw on the portfolio. He keeps two years of that gap in cash. He uses a
3.5% withdrawal rate in his own planning because he is young for this.

What he wants from a coach is confirmation and a watch: is the draw safe, how
to take it tax-efficiently (long-term gains in the 0% band, Roth later),
when to stop the job, and what the marketplace would cost if he did.

## What he would want the app to tell him

- That the plan works: $1,750 a month from $690,000 is a 3% draw, under his 3.5% rate, and the job covers the rest. The app instead shows a surplus of minus $1,838 and cash running out in December 2028, because it never draws on investments.
- A Barista FI number: what he needs given the income he keeps, (spending minus net pay) x 12 / 3.5% = about $600,000, which he has. The app's number covers all spending ($1,100,000) and says 63%.
- The tax on the draw: realized gains inside the 0% long-term band at his income.
- When the cash cushion needs topping up, and from which account.
- What quitting the job costs in premiums and what that does to the draw.
