# Splenda daddy: Devin Marsh (persona 03, 41, made up)

File: `households/03-splenda-daddy.json`. Walkthrough: `03-splenda-daddy-walkthrough.md`.

## Who he is

Devin is 41, single, an operations manager at a logistics company in Queens,
$86,400 a year. He is in an arrangement much like Raymond's, on a fraction of
the income: $1,500 a month in allowance plus about $400 in dinners, gifts and
weekend trips. He cannot really afford it. The Visa is at $9,800 of a $12,000
limit, there is an $8,000 personal loan he took last year to clear an earlier
card, and his savings are $3,600. He knows what the numbers say; what he
wants from a coach is not a lecture but a clear picture: what this costs him
per year, how long the card takes to clear if he stops, and whether there is a
version of this he can keep.

He is a realistic case of a lifestyle line funded on credit, and the app
should show that without judgment and without hiding it.

## What he would want the app to tell him

- What the arrangement costs a year, and what the Visa costs him in interest a year ($2,600 at 26.99%).
- The payoff date at the minimum, and with the $1,900 a month freed if he stopped.
- Whether the surplus is negative and by how much, without the number being hidden behind "rough".
- How close he is to the limit and what that does to his credit score.
- A plan that is not all or nothing: cap the line at $800 and see the date move.
