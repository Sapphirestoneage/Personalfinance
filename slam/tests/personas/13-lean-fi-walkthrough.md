## No field for

- Lean FI as the goal. The FI goal is one number; the app computes a separate "Lean FI" under its own definition (FAT tagged as needs, $1,540 a month: $462,000) and shows it as a footnote. Her definition (every food, housing and transportation cost, $1,660: $498,000) is neither the goal nor the footnote.
- "Reached": no flag or state says "you are past Lean FI"; the FI tile's percent is against the goal she typed.

## Forced into the wrong category

- Eating out ($120) is tagged food and want, so the app's Lean number drops it; hers keeps it. The "need" tag decides the Lean number, and the Ledger does not say so.

## Confusing labels or steps

- The red flag reads as a warning that she is doing it wrong. Her goal is below her spending on purpose; the gap is optional spending she can drop. The flag's "Next: spending of $1,666 a month or less" is in fact her plan.

## Results that look wrong or misleading

- The red stage on a Lean FI goal.
- "FI in about 0.5 years" is to her typed goal of $500,000 at the Likely set (6%); at her own 5% and her own lean number it is about a year. Both fine; the page shows one without saying which assumptions moved it.

## Assumptions that are not true for her

- Financial independence means covering all of today's spending. Lean FI means covering the essentials and choosing the rest.
- Food wants are not part of the essentials.

## Rating

**Works with workarounds** (type the goal, ignore the red flag, read Lean FI with the definition in mind).
