# Coast FIRE: Sofia Bianchi (persona 20, 36, made up)

File: `households/20-coast-fi.json`. Walkthrough: `20-coast-fi-walkthrough.md`.

## Who she is

Sofia is 36, in Syracuse, and spent her twenties and early thirties saving
hard as a consultant. With $310,000 invested she worked out that at 5% real
it grows to $1,000,000 by 65 on its own, and stopped: she now works part time
as a data analyst ($46,800) and contributes nothing more. The job covers her
$2,845 a month of spending with a little left over. She keeps $18,000 in cash.

Her question is whether the arithmetic holds and what would break it: a
market decade like 2000 to 2010, a move, a child, an illness, or simply
wanting more than $40,000 a year at 65.

## What she would want the app to tell her

- Coast FI reached, in plain words: $310,000 today is more than the $242,946 that grows to $1,000,000 by 65 at 5% real. The app shows this on the FI tile.
- Years to FI by growth alone (the app says 21.1, which is right) and the age that is (57).
- The worst case: the same at 4% real and a 3.5% withdrawal rate (the app's Worst set), which is $1,142,857 needed and a coast number of $370,000, so not reached. She would want that contrast on one screen.
- What a $200 a month contribution would buy back, in years.
- Health insurance and the Rule of 5 cushion (7.2 months, $20,484, which she has).
