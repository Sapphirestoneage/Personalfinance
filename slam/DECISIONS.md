# Decisions

One entry per decision: number, date, decision, why. Newest at the bottom.

| # | Date | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S-001 | 2026-10-15 | SLAM lives in a self-contained `slam/` folder on branch `claude/slam-budget-build-m26h38` of the existing repo, not in a new `slam` repo. Nothing outside `slam/` is read or changed. | The gh CLI in the build environment was not authenticated, so a new repo could not be created. The folder can be split into its own repo with one command (commands are in the build notes). |
| S-002 | 2026-10-15 | A blank Value is `amount: null, status: null` and shows as a "Blank" chip. The seven statuses are only for answered values. | Lesson 2 says every value carries a status, but "not asked yet" is different from "dont-know". Using null avoids inventing an eighth status word. |
| S-003 | 2026-10-15 | Undo and redo keep whole-document copies (up to 200 steps per open client), cleared when another client is opened. | Households are small, so copies are simple and cover every kind of change, including imports and month close. |
| S-004 | 2026-10-15 | Before any import, every household in the browser is copied into a backup (last 10 kept). Importing a file with an existing client id replaces that client. | "Automatic snapshot before any import" and importing your own export should act as a restore. |
| S-005 | 2026-10-15 | One single total is stored on an "Everything else" category. Category estimates entered later are carved out of it by calc, so the original total is never overwritten. | The brief says the total must survive being split later. |
| S-006 | 2026-10-15 | Default "need" flags: FAT lines (housing, groceries, transportation) plus debt minimums and health care. Eating out, delivery, and everything else default to want. Editable per line. | Strict FAT would make rent a need but insurance premiums and minimum payments wants, which skews 50/30/20. Listed as a question. |
| S-007 | 2026-10-15 | Closing a month locks a snapshot and moves to the next month. The live numbers carry forward (they describe a typical month) rather than being wiped. | Fixed costs like rent would otherwise need re-entry every month. Late entries go to the month's separate "revised" record. |
| S-008 | 2026-10-15 | Tax constants are for tax year 2026 (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32). Standard deductions and the lower brackets were checked against published summaries; higher brackets, head of household brackets, and the 184,500 wage base are from memory and flagged. | The brief asks for the current year if certain. The parts that matter for typical clients were verified. |
| S-009 | 2026-10-15 | Amounts are stored as typed (dollars, `amount: number`) and rounded only for display. Tests compare to the cent. | The brief defines `amount: number`. Storing what the client said keeps their own words exact. |
| S-010 | 2026-10-15 | A category's estimate and dream come from the category-level answer (the Rough pass) when there is one; otherwise from the sum of its line estimates. Line estimate fields are hidden when a category answer exists. | The client's original words are the anchor, and this way nothing is asked twice. |
| S-011 | 2026-10-15 | "Spending" means every category except Savings (Debt payments included). Surplus = take-home minus (spending + savings). Savings lines have no "% of spending". | Savings is money kept, not spent. The brief's savings rate and proof of cash both treat it separately. |
| S-012 | 2026-10-15 | Where a category has no actual, totals fall back to its estimate and name the categories that did ("basis"). A category with some line actuals uses only those (marked partial). | The brief asks for the fallback "and says so". Mixing per line inside a category would hide which number is real. |
| S-013 | 2026-10-15 | Take-home guess from gross: federal tax plus FICA (or self-employment tax for 1099 and side), standard deduction used once per household (sources with typed take-home use it first), each source taxed from the bottom bracket. No state tax, no credits, no QBI deduction, no extra Medicare. | Simple and conservative. Always labeled "guess" with the note "State tax not included". |
| S-014 | 2026-10-15 | Proof of cash compares entered spending (without debt payments) to take-home minus savings minus debt payments, and measures the gap against the implied number. Flag above 15%. | The paycheck is the harder fact; the flag asks the coach which side is right. |
| S-015 | 2026-10-15 | Blind spot = sum over spending categories of (actual above estimate, or the whole actual when there was no estimate) divided by total actual. It stays empty until at least one real actual exists. | Computed debt lines alone would otherwise show a misleading 0%. |
| S-016 | 2026-10-15 | Personal correction factor = the average actual/estimate ratio across closed months plus the current month. | With one month it equals the actual; with history it smooths out one-off months. |
| S-017 | 2026-10-15 | Completion weights: heavy 3 (take-home, housing, debt minimums, a single total), medium 2 (food, transportation, health), light 1 (the rest, dreams, gut, debt balance and APR). A category's line actuals share its weight. Known, roughly, not-for-me and computed count as done. | Matches the brief's heavy, medium, light list while keeping big categories with many lines from swamping the score. |
| S-018 | 2026-10-15 | Next three = weight x weakness (blank or dont-know 1.0, will-email 0.8, guess 0.7, roughly 0.3). Ties go to the bigger amount, then the order of the passes. Anything under 100 dollars a year goes to "small stuff". | Puts the heaviest unknowns first and still lets "roughly" numbers surface for firming up. |
| S-019 | 2026-10-15 | Single total: categories without their own estimate sit inside the total. Their actuals draw the total down instead of adding to it, and the blind spot compares them against the total. | Prevents double counting when a one-total client later gives some actuals. |
| S-020 | 2026-10-15 | Spending averages, cost-of-living tiers (HCOL 1.25, MCOL 1.0, LCOL 0.85) and every DRAFTT benchmark source are placeholders, marked as such in the data files and in the UI. | I could not verify the published figures. Listed as questions. |
| S-021 | 2026-10-15 | DRAFTT Retirement = after-tax retirement line plus pre-tax contributions, as a share of take-home. Taxes are a share of gross and only appear when every income source has a gross. | Keeps every share on take-home except the one labeled "of gross" (lesson 16). |
| S-022 | 2026-10-15 | Household size for averages = you, plus a spouse on a joint return, plus dependents (max 5). Roommates do not count. | Roommates run their own budgets. |
| S-023 | 2026-10-15 | Hourly take-home uses only income sources that have hours entered. | Mixing in pay without hours would overstate the hourly rate. |
| S-024 | 2026-10-15 | One-off amounts stay out of monthly totals (and out of the monthly mistake cost) and are listed separately. A ticket paid this month is entered as this month's actual. | The brief says one-offs are excluded from the monthly total. |
| S-025 | 2026-10-15 | The demo households ("Try with example numbers") are loaded from `test/households/*.json`, the same files the tests check. | One copy of the demo data, and the demos are always the hand-checked ones. |
| S-026 | 2026-10-15 | Amount boxes save when you leave the field. While typing, the running totals update from a scratch copy that is never saved. The screen re-renders after focus moves and puts focus back on the same field. | Live totals without rebuilding a form under the cursor, and one undo step per answer instead of per keystroke. |
| S-027 | 2026-10-15 | Closing a month also stores a small summary in `snapshots[]` for "since last time". | The brief asks for snapshots that later sessions can compare against; month close is the natural moment. |
| S-028 | 2026-10-15 | Blind-guess rounds are stored in `blindRounds[]` (id, date, estimates per category). Compatibility: documents without it load with an empty list. Added inside schema v1 because nothing has shipped yet. | The blind guess is the client's own words and must live in the number store, not on the screen. |
| S-029 | 2026-10-15 | "Doesn't apply" categories show only their answer and a way to change it, with no amount box. Mistake-tagged lines show a "Mistake" tag. Falling short on Savings shows red, not green. | Lessons 12 and 2. |
| S-030 | 2026-10-15 | A light, dark, or automatic theme switch sits in the top bar. Automatic follows the device. | Lets the coach check both modes on a call. Uses only the token file. |
| S-031 | 2026-10-15 | Charts are hand-drawn inline SVG (no charting library). Chart-ready numbers come from `src/calc/chart-data.js`; chart files only draw and word them. Hover any mark for its exact value. | Seven small primitives (bars, donut or pie, Sankey, gauge, ring, lines, scatter) cover every chart in the brief, with no dependency to pin. |
| S-032 | 2026-10-15 | Chart colors use an 8-slot categorical palette and an estimate / dream / actual / gut series set that were run through a colorblind and normal-vision separation checker in both light and dark mode. Category colors follow the category, never its rank. More than 7 categories fold into "Other". | Lesson 10 (one token file) plus charts that stay readable for colorblind clients. |
| S-033 | 2026-10-15 | Charts are drawn at the real pixel width of their card (re-drawn on resize), so labels stay 12px on a phone instead of shrinking. | Readable on a phone screen, per the brief. |
| S-034 | 2026-10-15 | The Budget screen shows four key charts (Sankey, estimate / dream / actual bars, spending donut, needs / wants / savings bar). Analyze holds every chart in four groups with a jump bar, so any chart is one tap away. | Lesson 7. |
