🗄 Everything You Own
🏛 Which Theatres Have Cups — Field Guide

Most Broadway houses sell a show-branded souvenir cup at the bar — roughly $15–25 with a drink — and in most houses that lidded cup is the only way a drink gets to your seat. Policies and prices change with every show, so this table is yours to keep current: tap Cups? to cycle what you found, and any cup you log from a show marks its house automatically.
