# Aesthetic audit, 2026-10-05

Already at the bar: the bones. Inter on an 8px rhythm, a quiet sapphire palette with no alarm colours, a 48px navy top bar, sticky table headers on a pale sapphire band, right-aligned tabular figures, and sentence case almost everywhere. The read-only views prove the system works: maya-spending-lines-client-1440-dark, maya-assumptions-coach-1440 and maya-scenarios-coach-1440 already look like a workpaper a planner would hand over. The one-pager's top half (name, date, key numbers, changes since last time) reads like a real client deliverable, and the client view's chart titles ("Where the money goes each month") are better than most commercial tools.

Three things hold the app back. First, the coach's tables are forms, not ledgers. Every cell is a bordered input, every row carries two bordered chips, and columns are cut off at the right edge, so the busiest screens (Spending, Accounts, Session) look like a database admin page instead of eMoney. Second, the screens say too much, too many times: the same seven areas are drawn as an orbit, a table and a sidebar column of percentages; "Starting soon" appears on seven screens; Measure is 4,569px of 40 ragged tiles; Learn repeats Measure's lenses; Accounts opens with twelve lines of "Inferred, not stored". Third, colour has lost its meaning: sapphire marks links, buttons, selection and rough numbers all at once, gold rings switch on at an arbitrary 90%, and in the dark theme anything painted with `--sapphire-900` turns near-white and becomes the loudest mark on the page. Fix those three and most screens move from 2 or 3 to 4 without a single new element.

## What does not look professional

### Layout and spacing

- **A1. The same seven areas are drawn three times on planet pages.** maya-income-coach-1440 shows an orbit (seven discs), a Row types table (the same seven rows with the same percentages) and the sidebar (the same percentage again). maya-taxes-coach-1440 draws a 600px orbit for a single row. Repetition reads as filler; professional tools show a thing once, in its densest form. Fix: on planet pages drop the orbit and make the Row types table the main 760px column, with the mapbar ("Income is at 89%", Open W-2 job) directly above it. Keep the orbit on Home only. See B2.
- **A2. "Starting soon" is squeezed into the side column.** On maya-income-coach-1440 and maya-taxes-coach-1440 the "What it does" text wraps to five lines and "Remove" is clipped to "Remov". Fix now: on planet pages render Starting soon in the main column under the table (the full-width version on maya-spending-lines-coach-1440 already fits on one line). Better: B1.
- **A3. Measure tiles are ragged.** maya-measure-coach-1440: tiles in one row are 76, 92 and 160px tall, Allocation sits alone on its own row, and the eye cannot run along a baseline. Cause: `.kpis { align-items: start; }` near the end of `ui/app.css`. Fix: delete that rule so the grid stretches each row to equal height. In `ui/views/measure.js`, when every metric in a group needs input, print the one-line sentence the Taxes group already uses on extreme-measure-coach-1440 ("Needs gross pay (4 numbers waiting).") instead of tiles; in a mixed group, drop the "Needs" tiles and add "3 waiting" to the group heading. Extreme shows ten "Needs ..." tiles today.
- **A4. Chart panels leave 200px holes.** maya-measure-coach-1440: FI progress (60px of content) is paired with Contribution waterfall, so its panel is 280px of white. Fix: reorder `CHARTS` in `engine/chartdata.js` to sankey, netWorth, balanceSheet, debtRace, runway, fiGauge, draftt, waterfall, taxes so short charts pair with short charts, and add `.chart-panel[data-chart="taxes"] { grid-column: 1 / -1; }` so the tax ladder does not sit alone in half a row.
- **A5. Client admin sits on every client's home.** maya-home-coach-1440: New client, Import, Load demo client, Export and Delete beside Maya's household, and a History table whose Now column is clipped ("~$240.00", "Retiremen"). Fix: merge Clients and History into one panel titled "Clients and history", list the open client first, cap History at six rows with "312 lines in the journal" as the link to the rest, and set `.history td.now { white-space: normal; }`. Show the Where column as plain slate text, not bordered chips.
- **A6. One-pager actions float away from the page.** maya-onepager-coach-1440: Edit and Print sit at the right edge of the window, 370px from the 816px sheet they act on. Fix: constrain that header to the sheet, for example `main > header:has(+ .onepager) { max-width: 816px; }`.
- **A7. Assumptions inputs drift away from their labels.** maya-assumptions-coach-1440: labels at x=249, inputs at x=655, then a 200px empty source column. Fix: `.assumptions { max-width: 560px; }` and `.assumptions .fieldrow { grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr) var(--field-w) auto; }`.
- **A8. A separate panel explains the cell shorthand.** Every ledger table is followed by a bordered panel reading "Pick a cell for its state and source. ~ rough, ? unknown, 1500-2000 range, send, 0 is a real zero." (`ui/table.js`, the `.fieldbar` near line 426). It looks like a leftover tooltip and adds one more box per page. Fix: render the field bar only while a cell is focused; when nothing is picked, render nothing. Move the shorthand legend into the ? sheet (`SHORTCUTS` in `ui/app.js`).
- **A9. Household facts carry noise.** maya-home-coach-1440: each fact has a "Verified" chip and a "Client" chip; the hint says "the state is New York until the client says otherwise" while the state is California; "More facts" is indented 8px from the label column. Fix: show that hint only when State is still the default, hide the default source chip (A17), and align "More facts" with the labels.

### Typography

- **A10. The heading scale inverts.** maya-onepager-coach-1440: section heads ("Key numbers", "To-dos", "Bring next time") are 12px semibold above 14px body text, so headings look like footnotes. Chart titles are 13px, panel titles 16px, and the global `h3` is 12px regular slate, which is why "Row types" on maya-income-coach-1440 reads as a stray caption. Eight sizes are in use (11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 20, 24, 32). Fix: `.onepager h3 { font-size: var(--fs-14); line-height: 20px; }`, `.onepager section { margin-bottom: var(--s-4); }`, `.chart-panel header h3, .metric-group > h3 { font-size: var(--fs-14); }`, and make "Row types" an `h2` inside the table's panel. Target scale: 12 meta, 13 tables, 14 body and section heads, 16 panel titles, 20 page titles, 32 the one hero number. Retire `--fs-24` (client page titles become 20px semibold).
- **A11. Keycaps and percentages crowd the sidebar.** Every nav item carries a percentage and an `Alt+1` keycap in monospace with a 2px bottom border, so the sidebar is three columns of text and reads like a developer tool. Linear and Stripe keep shortcuts in a help sheet. Fix: `.sidenav .kbd, .sidenav .fill-text { display: none; }` and `.sidenav a { grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr); }`. Drop the `Alt+N` keycap beside Add row too; put it in the button's `title`. The shortcuts stay in the ? sheet.
- **A12. Tildes and signs pile up.** extreme-measure-coach-1440 shows "~-$60,510" and "~-1.6%"; lens sentences say "roughly ~$2,550"; ranges read "-$37,650 to -$35,510", where the hyphen looks like a range dash. Fix in `engine/format.js` (`dollarsWhole`, `percent`): when a range line is shown under a KPI, drop the tilde from the headline, since the range already says it is rough. In `engine/lenses.js`, format numbers inside sentences with `rough: false`, because "roughly" already says it. Use the true minus sign (U+2212) instead of the hyphen-minus (format.js lines 36, 48 and 69).

### Colour and contrast

- **A13. Blue means four things.** Sapphire-700 marks links, primary buttons, selected nav and rough numbers. On maya-measure-coach-1440 more than half the headline numbers, the DRAFTT share list and the runway list are blue, so they read as links. On the one-pager the client's key numbers look clickable. Fix: `.kpi .value.rough, .rough-value, .input.rough, table.data td .input.rough { color: var(--ink); }`. The tilde and the range line already carry "rough" (the never-colour-alone rule holds). Sapphire then means "you can click this".
- **A14. Gold switches on at an arbitrary line.** maya-home-coach-1440: Taxes at 90% gets a gold ring and Income at 89% a blue one; the centre ring is gold beside "86% confident". The token file reserves gold for wins and unlocks. Fix: in `ui/orbit.js` lines 58 and 72 remove the `>= 90 ? ' gold'` branch so every ring is `--sapphire-500`; keep `amber` for attention.
- **A15. Three confidence numbers on one screen.** maya-home-coach-1440: the centre says 86%, the sidebar Home row says 99%, and the facts footer says "Confidence 99% across the facts". A client will ask which one is true. Fix: keep the centre figure only. A11 removes the sidebar figure; change the footer to "4 of 4 facts confirmed. 7 of 7 areas have rows."
- **A16. Chip borders everywhere.** maya-spending-lines-coach-1440 has 26 bordered chips; Source says "Client" on all 13 rows. maya-session-coach-1440 has a column of amber outlines. Boxes inside boxes are the main reason the tables feel busy. Fix in `ui/app.css`: `.chip.src, .chip.state-known, .chip.state-verified { border-color: transparent; background: transparent; padding: 0; }`, keeping borders only on the states that ask for action (rough, estimated, will send, unknown, verify). In `ui/chips.js`, render the default source (Client) as empty slate text, so only exceptions show.

### Tables and fields

- **A17. Every cell is a boxed input.** maya-spending-lines-coach-1440, maya-invest-accounts-coach-1440 and jordan-spending-lines-coach-1440 are grids of rectangles inside a grid of lines, so the ledger reads as a form. Compare maya-spending-lines-client-1440-dark, the same data as plain text, which already looks finished. Fix: `table.data td .input, table.data td .select { border-color: transparent; background-color: transparent; }`; the existing `:hover` and `:focus` rules then bring the border back exactly when it is useful. Hide the select arrow until hover: `table.data td .select { background-image: none; }`, restoring it in the `:hover` and `:focus` rules.
- **A18. Columns are cut off at the right edge.** "Unavoidab" (Spending), "Not applica" (Credit cards), "Stability" (W-2), "Not flag" (Goals) are clipped, with no visible cue that more exists, while 400 to 600px of page sits empty below. Fix: keep the table to six columns (Name, Amount, Per, State, Source, Category or the planet's equivalent) and move every other column into the row-details drawer now in progress in `ui/table.js` and the new `.drawer .row-details` rules. Also lower `--field-w-wide` from 176px to 160px.
- **A19. Placeholder words fill whole columns.** "Not applicable" is typed into Promo APR, Promo ends and Contribution % on every row (maya-debt-cards-coach-1440, maya-invest-accounts-coach-1440), and the Per column shows "pay" or "mo" in its own boxed select. Fix: render "not applicable" as a slate en dash (U+2013), the workpaper convention, and keep "Not entered" (empty is not zero) at 12px slate. Fold Per into the amount cell as the `.cell-money .select.cad` pill that is already styled, which removes a column.
- **A20. Every row has a checkbox with no visible use.** The `.pick` box sits in the Name cell of every row. Fix: `.ledger-table .pick { opacity: 0; }` and `.ledger-table tr:hover .pick, .ledger-table .pick:checked, .ledger-table .pick:focus-visible { opacity: 1; }`.
- **A21. 27 buttons that say "Mark done".** maya-session-coach-1440. A repeated text button is the heaviest possible control for a checkbox job. Fix: in `ui/views/session.js` line 82, render a `.pick` checkbox with `aria-label="Mark done"`; the column header "Done" stays.
- **A22. Number formats disagree.** jordan-debt-cards-coach-1440 shows Promo APR "0" beside "25.0%". Spending's status line shows "Gap: $16.67" where every other figure is whole dollars. History shows raw values "import" and "s2". Fix: a typed zero rate formats as "0.0%"; `ui/views/ledger.js` line 154 uses `dollarsWhole` for the gap; History maps "import" to "Imported" and "s2" to "Session 2".

### Charts

- **A23. Sankey labels collide.** maya-measure-coach-1440, maya-onepager-coach-1440 and maya-measure-coach-390: "Take-home $6,152" overprints "Food $800", "Utilities" is clipped to "tilities", and "Insurance $18" overprints "Debt payments $166". Overlapping labels are the fastest way to look amateur. Fix in `ui/charts.js` (sankey): fold every flow under 3% of gross into the existing "Smaller lines" node, set node padding to at least 14px, and print a right-side label only when its node is at least 12px tall.
- **A24. The shortfall flow looks like a barcode.** On extreme-measure-coach-1440, "Short this month" is drawn as vertical stripes, because `.sankey-link.kind-short { stroke-dasharray: 4 3; }` is applied to a 230px-wide stroke. Fix: remove the dasharray and use `stroke: var(--slate); stroke-opacity: 0.25;`. The label "Short" carries the meaning.
- **A25. Net worth to 95 shows the best case, not the plan.** maya-measure-coach-1440 and the one-pager: the y-axis runs to $14M because of the best-case band, so the likely line is squashed into the bottom fifth and the FI crossing is unreadable. "Enough to live on $1.3M" sits on the curve, and the caption is cut off ("after infla"). Fix in `ui/charts.js` (netWorth): set the y maximum to 1.25 times the likely line's maximum and clip the band with a `clipPath`; anchor the FI label at the left end of its dashed line; render the caption as an HTML `<p class="hint">` under the SVG instead of SVG text.
- **A26. Debt payoff has odd ticks and empty series.** maya-measure-coach-1440: ticks run Nov 2026, Feb 2028, May 2029, and the legend lists "Bilt $0". Fix: year ticks (`d3.timeYear.every(n)`, format `%Y`) and drop debts with a zero balance from both lines and legend.
- **A27. Legends look like filter buttons.** The bordered `.legend .chip` swatches ("Pre-tax $28,400", "and 2 more") look clickable. Fix: `.legend .chip { border: 0; background: none; padding: 0; height: 16px; }`. On Simulate, label the line ends directly ("Condo alone", "Four-day week alone"): the two "Each alone" lines share one colour today and cannot be told apart.
- **A28. Balance sheet bars show truncated text.** "Direct unsubsid" is cut inside a 96px bar, and the Taxable and HSA segments carry no label at all. Fix: in `ui/charts.js` near line 122, print an in-bar label only when the whole label fits, never a truncated one; the legend covers the rest.
