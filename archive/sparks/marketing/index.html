  1. Log every post on the Content screen: where, what, the hook, the ask. Come back in three days and type its numbers.
  2. Put people in on the People screen, by hand or from a CSV of your contacts. Each has a stage and a rhythm; the app says who to reach out to today, and how.
  3. Read this screen weekly. Targets, pace, the funnel and its worst step. Save the share report for your marketer friends.

Everything stays in this browser. Save a backup from time to time. No numbers are sent anywhere.
