More ways out: a spreadsheet, plain text, the app itself
Download the whole app

The spreadsheet opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets: a tab a door, money in money cells, a blank left blank. The app zip is the source from GitHub: unzip it and open index.html through a small local server (on a computer, python3 -m http.server in that folder, then localhost:8000). Your numbers are not in the zip; they stay in this browser and in your backup file.

One door for all of them. A backup, protected or plain; a household file; a Money Rooms spreadsheet you edited; a CSV from your bank. The page works out which it is and shows you what it would do before anything saves. One undo takes it all back.

Household file

Add it to what is here, or replace everything

Add keeps everything entered here and brings in what the file has that this does not: new accounts, debts, lines, people, without overwriting a number. Replace makes the file the household.

Money Rooms spreadsheet

Each line goes back to the row it names

Numbers written any way ($1,234.56, 24.99%, 6/3/2026, yes) are read. A blank cell leaves the row as it is; nothing becomes a zero.

Bank or card statement

Spending lines, dated from the file

Columns are matched once per bank and remembered. A line already in the log is skipped, so the same file twice changes nothing. The lines then sort by merchant in Expenses, where a category you choose becomes a rule.
