About you

Five numbers. They are what turn three of your six abilities from a guess into a measurement — and none of them leaves this browser. Rough is fine; you can change any of them later. Leave one blank if you do not know it — blank and zero are different answers and the sheet treats them differently.

Your six abilities

Twenty-seven points, every ability starting at 8, nothing above 15 — D&D Beyond's point buy, unchanged. Spend them on the three nobody can measure for you: Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma. Spend on Strength, Dexterity or Constitution too if you like, but the moment your money can measure one of those it takes over, and the next screen will show you it happening.

Every ability has three things underneath it, and every one of those has moves that genuinely shift it. Sorted by your weakest first, because that is where the cheap points are. Nothing here knows your situation and none of it is advice — it is what moves the number.

Last thing, then we play

Your six say what you're capable of. They don't say what to do next — and next is the whole game. There's an order to money stuff, and doing the right thing at the wrong time is how people lose years without ever making an obvious mistake.
