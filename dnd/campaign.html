About you

Five numbers. They are what turn three of your six abilities from a guess into a measurement — and none of them leaves this browser. Rough is fine; you can change any of them later. Leave one blank if you do not know it — blank and zero are different answers and the sheet treats them differently.

Your six abilities

Twenty-seven points, every ability starting at 8, nothing above 15 — D&D Beyond's point buy, unchanged. Spend them on the three nobody can measure for you: Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma. Spend on Strength, Dexterity or Constitution too if you like, but the moment your money can measure one of those it takes over, and the next screen will show you it happening.

Finish the three your money measures

Each answer completes one named sub-stat and nothing else — the page says which. None of it is required to play, and you can come back at any point.

Last thing: where the campaign starts you

Your six abilities say what you are capable of. They do not say what you should do next — and next is the whole game. That comes from a different thing entirely: a fixed order of operations, where each step only makes sense once the one before it is done.

Your numbers already place you on most of that order. Two answers finish it, and they are these because an unclaimed employer match is the one thing that outranks almost everything else — it is a pay rise you have already been offered. Everything else the ladder needs, it can read from what you have entered.
