The Monster Manual

Monsters are people and institutions whose incentives don't run with yours — whether or not they mean you harm. Some are predators. Some are entirely well-meaning and still bad for you, which is the harder category to see coming.

Hazards

Hazards aren't people — they're events, closer to weather than to an adversary. Nobody's incentives are against you here. They sort by where they originate: the environment around you, your relationships, or your own body.

    Status Effects

    Conditions, not stat changes. A Disabled character's INT, WIS and CHA are untouched — what changes is which actions, feats and multiclass options are available, the same way being Charmed doesn't lower your Wisdom, it just stops you acting on it freely.

    Feat Trees

    Protection, Tax Strategy and Legacy/Giving were each tried as classes and correctly rejected: they aren't ways of moving money, they're tools layered on a build that already exists. Any character, in any class, can pursue any of these.
