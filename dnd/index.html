How do you want to build?

Six ability scores, like any character sheet. Three of them — knowing the rules, judgment, and dealing with people — have no dollar figure behind them, so they are always yours to decide. The other three are earning power, resilience and savings: you can buy those too, and they stand until your real numbers replace them. Every route lands on the same scale.

You can switch at any time. Nothing is lost.

Your Level is how far you are through your own number, not a dollar target. HP is how many weeks you could go with no income. AC is what stops a hit landing at all. Your class may well change too, because a class here is a lever you actually pull, not a temperament.

It also fills in the three saves this page had to leave blank — and then you can actually run the things above. Not just which of them hunt you: whether they land, how hard, and how many weeks it costs you when they do.
