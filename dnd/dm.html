DM mode

Build an encounter for someone else. Set what the target looks like, pick what comes at them, and see what happens. Nothing here touches your own character — the whole scenario lives in the link, so sharing it is copying the address bar.

The target

Bills and lawsuits attack this; pitches and panics go around it. Leave it blank and the attackers report unknown rather than assuming no cover.

Leave one blank and it counts as unscored, exactly as it would on a real sheet — the creature simply cannot target it.

What they have

Held, missing, or not established. Not established applies nothing — a DM who has not decided is not the same as a target who lacks it.
