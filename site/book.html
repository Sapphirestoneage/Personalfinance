What to bring

Nothing, honestly. But if you want a head start:

  • What you spend in a typical month, roughly. Rent or mortgage, food, getting around, everything else.
  • What you have saved and invested, roughly. One total is fine.
  • What you owe, if anything, and the rate on the one that bothers you most.
  • The question on your mind this year. A house, a job change, a kid, a sabbatical, "can we stop at fifty".

Want to see a number before the call? Your FIRE number takes three figures and stores nothing.

What happens after

Either a session, or a tool, or nothing

If a session would help, we book it on the call. If a free tool is enough for where you are, Eli says so and points you at it. If it is not a fit, you still leave with your number. There is no follow-up sequence and no list you get added to.
