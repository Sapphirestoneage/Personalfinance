The free call

  1. You talk.

    Where you are, what is on your mind this year, what "sorted" would feel like.

  2. Eli asks.

    Roughly what comes in, roughly what goes out, roughly what is saved. Rough is fine; the call is about finding which numbers matter.

  3. One number.

    Your first reading: the work-optional date as a band, or the month's surplus, or how long the cushion lasts. Whichever your situation needs first.

  4. You decide.

    If a session would help, we book it. If a free tool is enough, Eli says so and points you at it.

    The plan stays alive

    Most plans die in the drawer. Yours lives on a screen you can open on your phone: a life map from today to the work-optional date with every goal placed on it, each goal's monthly number and status, what changed since last time, and your homework. Once a month you send what moved, and Eli updates the map and writes back.

    Where your numbers live. In Eli's coaching console on his own device, sealed with a passphrase, one file per client. Never in a shared spreadsheet, never on a server. When you stop coaching, you get your file and Eli deletes his copy.
