E

Eli Saperstein, money coach.

Eli coaches people toward financial independence, and he built the software he coaches with. That second part is the whole point: on a call, every number you see is computed from your figures by one set of formulas, with the assumptions written on the page, and nothing is invented in to make the picture prettier.

Why this exists

Most money stress is not knowing the number

People are rarely stressed because a number is bad. They are stressed because they do not know it, or because two spreadsheets disagree, or because the one figure that decides everything, what is left at the end of a normal month, has never been written down. Once the number is on the screen, the conversation changes. It stops being about feelings and starts being about the next thing to do.

So Eli's coaching starts with getting the number on the screen. The software he built, SPARKS, exists because the shared spreadsheet he used to coach from could not keep every figure honest: a blank got treated as a zero, a formula got copied and drifted, a client's rent got updated in one tab and not another. SPARKS holds one household, gives every number exactly one owner, and computes every reading from the same file. On a call, Eli drives it live and you watch your future update as you talk.

How he works

The rules, and they are enforced by tests

  • Empty is not zero. A blank box means "not entered". A reading that needs a figure you have not given says so instead of inventing one.
  • One number, one owner. Every shared figure is typed in exactly one place and read everywhere else, so there is never a second copy to drift.
  • One formula, one function. The FIRE number on this site, in the app, and on Eli's console is the same code.
  • Everything in today's dollars. Every projection uses a return above inflation, so the numbers you see are numbers you would recognise.
  • Every number says how old it is. A reading built on a figure from months ago says so.
  • Nothing leaves the device. The free tools keep your numbers in your own browser. Clients' numbers live sealed on Eli's device, one file each, never on a server, never in a shared sheet.
  • No real household in public. The software is open source. The only figures in it belong to Robin Sparks, who is made up.
Honest limits

What coaching is not

  • Not investment advice. Eli is a coach, not an investment adviser. He will not pick funds for you, and he will say when a question needs someone with a licence.
  • Not tax preparation. The tax rooms estimate; a preparer files. The reference tables are United States figures for 2026.
  • Not a product. There is nothing to buy on this site except time with a person. Nothing is sold, recommended or referred for a commission.
