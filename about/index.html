About

Hi, I’m Eli.

I grew up around people I love who quietly panicked about money, and I noticed early how far that panic travels into everything else.

I studied accounting at Yeshiva University (BS and MS) and started my career auditing financial services firms at Deloitte. I learned the mechanics thoroughly, and I learned something less expected: the people who were okay weren’t the ones who knew the most. They were the ones who’d stopped being afraid to look.

So I started sitting down with friends and looking with them. That became {{CLIENT_COUNT}} people, then a practice. Along the way I built the FI Address, a way of describing where someone is financially in four characters, which led to working with Jonathan Mendonsa and ChooseFI, and to a conversation on the BiggerPockets Money podcast. I’m CPA-eligible and working toward the CFP® credential.

I don’t sell products or manage assets. I coach, I build free tools, and I write occasionally. I live in Rockland County, New York, and I’m a Broadway person, which is relevant only in that I believe the boring middle of most plans can be cut.
