Coaching

Coaching, if you want a person in the room.

Everything on this site is free to use on your own. Coaching is for when you’d rather not do it alone, or when the situation is tangled enough that a second set of eyes changes the answer.

Free conversation 60 min · free

We look at your real numbers, together, without any judgment. You’ll leave with a one-page picture of where you stand and the next three moves in order. There’s no pitch at the end. Some people book a package afterward; most don’t need to.

The intake form is optional. If filling it out is the thing that’s stopping you, skip it and just come.

What to expect — thirty seconds’ worth

  1. You pick a time on the calendar. Video by default; phone if you’d rather.
  2. There’s an intake form. It is optional. Coming with nothing is fine.
  3. We spend the hour on your actual numbers, whatever state they’re in.
  4. You leave with the one-page picture and the next three moves, in order.
  5. Nothing is sold at the end. If a package would help, I’ll say so once.

How I work

  • No products, no commissions, no assets under management. You’re the only person paying me, so I only work for you.
  • I’m an accountant by training (Deloitte, MS in Accounting) and I’m working toward the CFP® credential. I’ll show my math.
  • Plans are written to be done, not admired. If a step takes more than an evening, I’ve written it wrong.
  • Everything is confidential.

Questions people ask first

Is this financial advice?

It’s coaching and education. I don’t manage money or sell anything. If you need a registered advisor, I’ll tell you.

What if my situation is embarrassing?

Then it’s like most situations. I’ve seen it, and it’s fixable.

Do I need to prepare?

No. Bring whatever you have, even if it’s nothing.

Do you work with people outside New York?

Yes, most sessions are video.
