Move the Debt

Pay Cash or Finance

0% for 24 months, or 5% off for cash. Which leaves you richer after the last payment, and the back interest a deferred plan hides.

Asks for: price, the offer, where your cash sits 05

Lump Sum or Payments

A pension buyout, a severance, a settlement. The return the payments quietly promise, and the age the lump sum runs out if you take it.

Asks for: the two offers, your age, your return 06

Cash Ladder

CDs or Treasury bills in rungs against one savings account: the blended rate, what unlocks when, and the tax-equivalent edge of a Treasury.

Asks for: the cash, the rungs, the account rate 07

Three Paychecks

Paid every two weeks means two months a year hold a third check. Which months, how much, and the budget that never counts on it.

Asks for: how often, next payday, net pay 08

Borrow From Yourself

A 401(k) loan looks free because the interest comes back to you. What the money did not earn while it was out, and the bill if you leave the job.

Asks for: the loan, your balance, rates, term 09

Sinking Funds

The yearly bills that arrive all at once, spread into a monthly set-aside. What the fund should hold today and what lands in each of the next twelve months.

Asks for: each bill, how often, when next 10

Stay or Move

The rent went up. Over the lease you would sign, does moving pay for itself, and what is the counter-offer that makes staying a wash?

Asks for: the rents, the moving costs, the term
