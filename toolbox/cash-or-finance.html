You are assumed to hold the price in cash. Paying cash: the discount stays in the account and earns the parked rate. Financing: the account keeps earning while a payment leaves it each month and any fee leaves at once. Whichever leaves more in the account after the last payment won. The payment is the level payment for the APR and term. This tool stores nothing.
