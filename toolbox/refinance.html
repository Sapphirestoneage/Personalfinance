The payment on each loan is the level payment for its rate and term (engines/projection.js). Every path is run month by month to its last payment; interest is rounded to the cent each month. Closing costs count in the new loan's cost whether paid up front or rolled in. Taxes, escrow, insurance and points are not modelled; a mortgage interest deduction is not either. This tool stores nothing.
