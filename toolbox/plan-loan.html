Fill in the loan to see what it costs.

The cost of a plan loan is the growth the borrowed money misses: what it would have become left in the plan, less what the repayments become as they land back in and grow. Paused contributions are counted at what they would have grown to; a lost employer match is not counted, and would make it worse. On leaving a job the unpaid balance is due; a plan may allow rolling the offset to an IRA by the tax deadline. The limits come from toolbox/data/plan_loan_rules_2026.json. This tool stores nothing.
