The cash, and where it sits now
The rungs

A term and its rate. Leave a rung blank to skip it. The cash is split evenly unless you give shares.

The answer

Fill in the cash, the account rate and at least one rung.

Each rung earns simple interest to its maturity; the year figure is the cash times the spread between the ladder's blended rate and the account. A Treasury's tax-equivalent rate is its rate divided by one minus your state rate, because its interest is exempt from state income tax and the account's is not; the comparison uses it. Rates you type are taken as annual yields. Early withdrawal penalties on CDs, reinvestment at maturity and federal tax are not modelled. This tool stores nothing.
