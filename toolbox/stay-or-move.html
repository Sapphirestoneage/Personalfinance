Both paths are summed over the lease term you name. Staying costs the new rent each month. Moving costs the new place's rent plus the monthly changes, plus the one-off costs on day one. The counter-offer is the rent at which staying would cost exactly what moving does over the term. A returned deposit is not a cost, so do not enter it; the hassle of moving is real and has no box. This tool stores nothing.
