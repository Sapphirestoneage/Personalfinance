The implied rate is the yearly return at which the lump sum, invested today, would fund exactly these payments to the age you plan to. The run-out age takes the lump, grows it at your rate compounded monthly, and draws the payment from the start age. Nothing here knows how long you will live, whether the payer stays solvent, or what tax either path carries: a lump sum rolled to an IRA is not taxed on arrival; a payment is taxed as it comes. This tool stores nothing.
