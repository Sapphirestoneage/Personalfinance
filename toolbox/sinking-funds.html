The fund

Add a bill to see the set-aside.

Each bill's set-aside is its amount over the months between one and the next, rounded up to the cent. What the fund should hold today is the share of each bill that has "accrued" since the last one: a yearly bill due next month is nearly all owed already. The months ahead start from the amount the fund should hold, add the set-aside and take out what lands. The list of bills is kept in this browser under its own key and nowhere else; nothing is written to your household.
