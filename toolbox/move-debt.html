The trap is not in the arithmetic. A card moved to 0% is an empty card. If it fills again, you have two balances, and this page has no box for that. Close it, freeze it, or cut it up.

The cost of a path is everything paid minus the balance itself: interest plus fees. Each path is run month by month at your payment (the loan at its own payment), interest rounded to the cent. The transfer fee goes onto the balance; the promo is taken at 0% and the after-rate applies to whatever is left. Minimum payments, late fees, credit score effects and the cost of a hard pull are not modelled. This tool stores nothing.
