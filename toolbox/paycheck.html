Every check is assumed to look like this one. The year's federal tax is the SPARKS tax engine on the year's wages after the pre-tax deductions and the standard deduction; no credits, no itemising, no other household income unless you type it. Payroll tax is Social Security up to the wage base plus Medicare. The state figure applies the state schedule to federal taxable income and ignores state deductions and credits. An estimate, not a return; the W-4 is where the change is made. This tool stores nothing.
