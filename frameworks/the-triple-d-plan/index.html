A worked example

The What If, Life room runs every event three ways from one set of numbers. Dream: returns at the 75th percentile, income 10% higher afterward, no gap. Default: median returns, income unchanged, the event’s usual gap. Disaster: 25th-percentile returns, income 15% lower, the gap three times as long, and a worst-plausible-year shock on top. A six-month sabbatical then shows as three net-worth lines, and the point is the spread between them, not any one of them.

Taken from how the room implements it, so it cannot drift from the tool.
