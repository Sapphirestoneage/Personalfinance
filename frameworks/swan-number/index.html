A worked example

The Sleep At Night room stores a self-reported target and shows it next to the computed emergency fund. With $3,000 a month of expenses and $9,000 in cash, the maths says three months of cover. If your SWAN Number is six months, that is $18,000, and the room shows the $9,000 gap — without telling you the maths is right and you are wrong.

Taken from how the room implements it, so it cannot drift from the tool.

The room that runs it

Sleep At Night — Asks for your number, in dollars or months, and puts it beside the emergency-fund figure the maths produces. Neither overwrites the other.
