Privacy

What this site keeps, and where.

Short, because there isn’t much to say.

On this site

The pages of stresslessaboutmoney.com store two settings in your browser’s local storage: whether you chose light or dark mode, and whether calm mode is on. That is all. There are no cookies, no analytics, no accounts, and no third-party scripts, with one exception below.

The email list

If you give your email address in the form at the bottom of the Notes page, it goes to the email provider named in that form and nowhere else. You get one short email when a new tool is finished. Unsubscribing is one click in any of them. {{EMAIL_PROVIDER_NAME}} is the provider; its own privacy policy applies to the address you give it.

The Money Rooms

The tools keep the numbers you type in your own browser’s local storage, on your own device. Nothing you type is sent to me or to anyone else; the pages make no network request that carries your data. If you copy a share link, your numbers are encoded inside that link itself — anyone you send it to can open them, so treat it the way you would treat a photo of the page. If you export a file, the file is yours and lives wherever you saved it.

Clearing your browser’s site data for this domain removes everything the tools stored. There is no copy anywhere else to remove.

Coaching

Anything you share in a session or on an intake form stays between us. The booking calendar is run by {{BOOKING_PROVIDER_NAME}}, and its privacy policy covers the details you enter there.
