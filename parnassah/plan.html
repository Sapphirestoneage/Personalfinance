      The tuition years, and after

      Modern Orthodox families save for retirement late because tuition takes the years when compounding would have done the most. The bars are every tuition year ahead; the line is what goes to retirement now. Below, what the freed tuition could grow to if it kept going into savings the year the last bill is paid.

      Nothing leaves this device. There is no account and no server; a backup is a file you save on purpose.

      Empty is not zero: a blank box means "not entered", and a figure that needs it says so instead of guessing.

      The reference figures are dated starting points of low confidence, not quotes. Nothing here is halachic, tax or legal advice: bring the numbers to your rav, your preparer and your lawyer.
