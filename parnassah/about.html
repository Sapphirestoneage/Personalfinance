How I work

    Your numbers, your decisions

    I do not sell anything but my time. No products, no portfolio, no commission. You decide; I make sure you can see what you are deciding.

    Plain words

    Every number on the screen has a sentence beside it saying what it means and where to find it. If a term needs a glossary, I have failed.

    Empty is not zero

    A number we do not know yet stays blank, and the picture says so. I would rather show you an honest gap than a tidy guess.

    The rav has the last word

    Maaser, inheritance, a heter iska: I do the arithmetic and send you to your rav with a number, never a ruling.

    Nothing leaves this device. There is no account and no server; a backup is a file you save on purpose.

    Empty is not zero: a blank box means "not entered", and a figure that needs it says so instead of guessing.

    The reference figures are dated starting points of low confidence, not quotes. Nothing here is halachic, tax or legal advice: bring the numbers to your rav, your preparer and your lawyer.
