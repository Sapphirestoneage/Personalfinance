The pages

How this works

  1. Type what you know. A blank box is "not entered", never zero. Every box has a ? that says what it means, where to find it, and what to do if unsure.
  2. Start from a typical figure where you have none. Tuition, camp, a wedding: the site carries dated bands, marked as assumed until you replace them.
  3. Read the picture. Each page draws its numbers and says what they mean. The last page adds them all up.

Not sure where to begin? Try with example numbers above fills every page with the Adlers, an invented family of six in Teaneck. Nothing about them is real.

Nothing leaves this device. There is no account and no server; a backup is a file you save on purpose.

Empty is not zero: a blank box means "not entered", and a figure that needs it says so instead of guessing.

The reference figures are dated starting points of low confidence, not quotes. Nothing here is halachic, tax or legal advice: bring the numbers to your rav, your preparer and your lawyer.
