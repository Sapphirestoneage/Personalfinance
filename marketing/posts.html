The log

Importing posts from a spreadsheet what the columns can be called

A header row, then one row a post. The reader finds columns by name: date, channel (or platform), format, topic (or pillar), hook (or title), cta, link, minutes, and the results reach (or impressions, views), likes, comments, shares, saves, clicks, dms, follows, leads. Anything else is ignored. A blank result stays blank.
