Importing contacts Google Contacts, LinkedIn connections, any sheet

A header row, then one row a person. Columns are found by name: name (or first name and last name), email, phone, company (or organization), role (or position, title), platform, handle (or url), source, stage, tags (or labels), notes, and a date column such as connected on. LinkedIn's connections export and Google's contacts export both read as they are. People already here (same email, or same name and company) are skipped.
