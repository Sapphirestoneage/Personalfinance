The organizations below exist for exactly the situations on the other pages. Every one of them is free to ask. If a link has moved, the name still searches.

Three asks that are normal

"Do you have a sliding scale?" Every synagogue, most therapists, many camps. The answer is nearly always yes.

"Is there an emergency fund?" Your Federation and Jewish Family Service have one. So does your shul, quietly.

"Would you be my guarantor?" That is how a gemach works. It is a compliment to be asked.
