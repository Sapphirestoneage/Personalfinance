Three rules

Empty is not zero. A blank box is "not entered yet" and the page says so. It never becomes a number on its own.

The guide figure is not your figure. Beside every box is a typical range and sometimes a starting number. Press "use this" and it becomes yours; otherwise it is a hint.

Nothing leaves this browser. There is no account and no server. Clear your numbers with one button. The example household is invented.

Why these pages

A Jewish year has its own calendar of bills. A queer household often has no legal default that matches its shape. Making a family or getting care can cost more than a car and less than a house, and is planned like neither. And the tradition already holds the answers for two of the hardest parts: a rate for giving, and a loan without interest.

This sits beside Money Rooms, the household app, and reads none of its numbers.

Nothing on this site is legal, tax or medical advice. Every figure is arithmetic on what you typed, beside a guide range with its source named on each page.
