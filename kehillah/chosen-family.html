A cushion for leaving safely

If a home, a job or a family could stop being safe, the number that matters is how many months you could cover on your own. Three months of the bare minimum is the usual first goal. Type the lean month: rent, food, transport, medication, nothing else.

Keep it in an account in your name only, at a bank the household does not share, with paperless statements. A gemach can bridge a gap this pot cannot; the Gemach page compares.
