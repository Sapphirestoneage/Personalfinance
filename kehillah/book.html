What happens on the call

  1. You say what is going on. In any words. No numbers needed, though you can bring them.
  2. I say what I would do first. One step, this week, whether or not we work together.
  3. We decide if there is a next call. If coaching would help, I say which shape and we settle a price on the sliding scale. If it would not, I say that too.
