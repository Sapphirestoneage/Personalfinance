Taking stock

Cheshbon ha-nefesh, with a bank statement open.

When you last sat with this

Once a year, in Elul

Or whenever the year turns for you. Answers save as you go; there is no score.

Each new moon

Four questions at Rosh Chodesh, or on the first of the month. Short answers.

A Shabbat from money

One day a week with no banking app, no shopping, no bill. For those who keep Shabbat it is already the rule; for those who do not, it is the cheapest financial habit there is. Nothing to enter: only to keep.
