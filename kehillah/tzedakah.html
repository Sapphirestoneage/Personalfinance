A tenth is the custom. The number is yours.

Tzedakah is from the word for justice: a share owed, not a tip. The tradition sets a tenth as the norm, a fifth as the ceiling, and less when money is tight. Choose the rate and the income it applies to, and the page does the arithmetic and keeps the tally.

The ladder of giving

Maimonides ranked eight ways to give. The rung is about how, not how much; the top rung is a loan or a job, which is what a gemach is. The count is how many of your gifts sit on each rung.
