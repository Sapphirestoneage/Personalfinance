The safeword is the fund that lets you say no: to a client, a scene, a platform's new terms, a bank's letter. It is sized for this life, not an office job: your lean month plus the house's fixed costs, for as many months as you choose, with a buffer when one platform holds your rent and another when nothing you hold is beyond a freeze.

Your own month

The lean month is food, home, transport, meds, the phone: what it costs to stay housed and fed with every extra cut. The typical month is what you actually spend. Both are yours, not the practice's; the house has its own page.
