How booking works

  1. Book. Pick a time, or email me with the name you go by and what you do. No legal name, no intake form, no "tell me about your goals" essay.
  2. Before the call. If you want a head start, open the planner and fill in the Streams page. Three months of payout statements or a rough count of the cash is plenty.
  3. On the call. Video, your screen or mine. We fill the planner together and read what it says. You leave with the first three moves written down.
  4. After. A recap the same day. For the package, the messages start the same week and the next call is on the calendar before we hang up.
