What the platform does not tell you

  • Their cut is not your only cut. Twenty percent to the site, a processing fee under that, a currency conversion on top, a chargeback held against you. The planner takes the typical month and says what actually lands.
  • A held balance is a loan you did not agree to. Money the site shows you but has not sent is theirs until it lands. Take payouts on a schedule and keep the balance small.
  • The apps forbid the work. Most peer-to-peer payment apps ban adult services in their terms. A closure there comes without warning and sometimes with the balance held. Never let one be your only rail.
  • Tributes are income. Gifts, drains, wishlist items with a cash value. The jar does not care what it was called.
  • Findom needs a floor too. If you are the one receiving, the protocol page checks the giver's floor, the cap and the safeword, because a drain that eats someone's rent is not the practice you want your name on.
  • Content outlives the login. The digital file: where the accounts, the wallet keys and the two-factor codes are, and who may use them if you cannot.
