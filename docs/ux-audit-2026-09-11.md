# UX audit, 2026-09-11

Every page the owner can open (94: the home page, the map and 92 rooms),
checked four ways: a static scan of the markup, a headless phone (iPhone 13
width) opened on every page twice (a fresh browser, then a seeded household,
`fixtures/households/dink-highearn.json`) with every visible button tapped,
phone and desktop screenshots of the doors, and a code read of every room.
Line numbers are as of commit d4cfe92.

## Broken and dead (verified in the browser or the code)

1. **The One-Pager's "Save a private file" throws.** `rooms/one-pager.html:160`
   calls `RoomExport.download`, but `shared/roomexport.js:215` only exports
   `_download`. The button does nothing and logs an error. (Shipped in D-219.)
2. **Ledger "(missing)" links go nowhere.** A computed row's "Worked out from
   … (missing)" links to `#row-<id>` (`rooms/ledger.html:290`) but rows carry
   `data-row`, not an id (`:271`). Three to five dead links per state.
3. **Four cross-room anchors do not exist:** Expenses "Lean FI" →
   `fire.html#lean` (`rooms/expenses.html:565`); The Score "Add your date of
   birth" → `start.html#q-dob` (`rooms/health.html:214`); Roth Conversions
   "Turn it on in Settings" → `settings.html#features` (`rooms/roth-aca.html:63`);
   Credential hours → `real-hourly-wage.html#hours` (`rooms/credential.html:361`).
   Each lands at the top of the page instead.
4. **Start Here "Read a screenshot"** needs `window.TextDetector`
   (`rooms/start.html:922`), which no shipping browser has. It always answers
   "This browser cannot read text out of a picture."
5. **Settings switches that say "Not shown anywhere yet"** can still be
   toggled (`rooms/settings.html:90`).
6. **"Save or print this room"** at the foot of every room is a folded
   `<details>` whose summary is styled as an eyebrow label; on the phone it
   reads as a dead caption, not a control (`shared/roomexport.js:186`).

## Too much on screen

  repeat block (Debts, Accounts) is drawn once under every level that holds
  one of its rows, so the Debts form appears under level 2 and again under
  level 3 (`rooms/express.html:259-278`); it still asks for debt blocks after
  "Any debt: No"; and read-only computed rows ("Net worth $440,000",

## Too many questions

## Not intuitive

- **The dashboard's "next thing money should do"** for a seeded household
  with $440,000 and no debt is "The panel needs highest deductible before it
  can place you. Answer it in Start Here." The ladder cannot place anyone
  without it, and the fallback picks the first missing Start Here field
  (`index.html:834-841`), sending people to the room slated for retirement.
- **The Ledger home** asks "Which one do you want to go into now?" with six
  tiles and "You understand 43% of your financial picture" for a household
  that has entered everything the dashboard needs. Its empty state does not
  point at the First Round. On-screen labels are developer notes: "The nine
  spheres, the older order (kept while the doors settle)", "Beyond the nine:
  What Matters. No numbers there.", "Share progress, not balances →". The
  prev/next bar on the home reads "← Your Data … The One-Pager →".
- **"The next thing to unlearn"** shows a struck-through headline.
- **Private vocabulary on screen**: "the doors", "DRAFTT", "FOO step",
  "block", "Freeze", "sphere", "virtue", "Stack it / Stacked ✓", "fogged /
  warp / boost / the fortress line" (Skill Tree), "Earner / Keeper / Builder /
  Compounder / Landholder / Anchor / Speculator" (Stacker), "Mint-proof",
  "The leak line", "Filed under … signed by nobody", "Sheet A3 of the drawing
  set", "Priced in life" beside "Through the lens".
- **Ledes that do not match the page**: Financial Snapshot "Ten numbers in.
  Nine out" on a page with 13 cards and no inputs (`:119`); Quick Math "Four
  small answers" above five cards (`:69`).
- **Feedback that vanishes**: "Saved. Snapshot taken." is overwritten by an
  instant redirect (`refresh.html:315`, `comeback.html:182`). FIRE says
  "Previewing 1 changed assumption. Reload to go back" with no reset button
  (`fire.html:446`). Budget's "What if… (a view that saves nothing)". Goals'
  "Or one total … if not itemised" never says which wins. Reversibility
  shows "Answer 1 / 2 / 3" slots until a decision is picked.
- Accessibility, from the existing `tests/reports/a11y.json`: 11 rooms fail
  colour contrast (16 nodes), 3 skip a heading level.

## Efficiency

- **33 rooms call `Reference.load()` with no list** and fetch all 87 data
  files on open (`shared/reference.js:174`; e.g. `credit.html:294`,
  `fire.html:509`, `unlearning.html:279`, `cash-flow.html:548`).
- **Whole-page re-render on every keystroke, no debounce**: Down Payment
  (`:135`), First Car (`:137`), FIRE Lab (a 20-cell sensitivity grid per
  pixel of slider drag, `:426`), Middle Class Trap (four projections per
  keystroke, `:184`), Offer Compare (an FI projection per offer per
  keystroke, `:162`), Skill Tree (a 625-node board per click, `:545`),
  Credential (two change handlers, paints twice, `:440,461,468`).
- **The same sum several times per paint**: Rollover runs `costOf` (two tax
  estimates) five times (`:507-685`); Big Purchase `weigh` four times; Income
  `Ledger.month` fourteen times (`:561-612`); Debt Payoff four strategies
  plus three simulations (`:1345,1390,1598`).
- **Dead code**: Cash Flow computes `left` then overwrites it (`:225,231`) and
  builds `due` then filters it to nothing (`:497-499`); Financial Snapshot's
  unused constants (`:335-338`); a no-op ternary in Real Hourly Wage (`:152`).
- **Script weight**: the dashboard loads 47 scripts; Get Help loads 12 for
  static text; History loads 11 engines to press one button; the Statement
  26; the Stacker 33; What If Life 37. Income loads projection, tier0 and
  cashflow and uses none.
- **Duplicate code**: Refresh and Welcome Back; the four 20/3/8 tests.
