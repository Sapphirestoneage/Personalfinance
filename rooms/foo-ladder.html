What the numbers say

    How the scale works

    Paying down a debt saves its rate, for certain. Capturing a match brings the employer's cents on the dollar at once. Investing earns whatever the decade brings: the middle of the historical range is shown with its low and high, from data/return_bands.json. The order of operations is a note on each line, never a re-sort: the scale is the return, the ladder is the framework.

    A bonus, an inheritance, a sale. Put it in all at once, or feed it in over a few months? There is a clean answer to when spreading it wins, and it is not the one most calculators give.
