Your Data

It lives in this browser. This is every way it comes in or goes out: a file, a link, or a pasted statement sorted into the right places.

Take it with you

A file, or a link

There is no account and no server. A share link keeps everything after the #, which a browser never sends to any site.

Load a file

Replace what is here, or add to it

A file this app downloaded, from this browser or another. Replace makes it the household. Add keeps everything entered here and brings in what the file has that this does not — new accounts, debts, lines, people — without overwriting a number.

Paste in new numbers

A line each, sorted into where it belongs

A statement, a note to yourself, a CSV. Each line with an amount is placed as a debt, an account, a monthly expense, or pay — and you can change any placement before it is taken. One undo takes the whole lot back.
