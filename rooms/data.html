Your Data

It lives in this browser. This is every way it comes in or goes out: a file, a link, or a pasted statement sorted into the right places.

Take it with you

A file, or a link

There is no account and no server. On a phone, Send opens the share sheet with your numbers as a small file: mail it to yourself, message it, drop it in a drive, then load it on the other device. A share link works too, though it is long, and most chat apps cut it off. This file is the whole backup, written for the app to read back, not for you: the one made to be read is the spreadsheet below.

Bank CSV, on-device

Drop in a statement download

A CSV from your bank or card site is read here, in this browser; nothing is sent. Columns are matched once per bank and remembered. Every line is shown before anything saves; spending lands in the log with its date from the file; a line already in the log is skipped, so the same file twice changes nothing.

Mint-proof

A spreadsheet, and the app itself

Nothing here can be taken away. Download the spreadsheet gives an Excel file that opens as a sheet in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets: a tab a door (Debt, Assets, Income, Taxes, Expenses, You), money in money cells, a percent as a percent, a date as a date, headings in words. A blank stays blank and "not sure yet" is words, never a zero. The code is public too: the whole app comes down as a folder.

Download the whole app

Bring the sheet back in

The same file, edited anywhere, comes back here: an Excel file or a CSV, either way. Each line goes to the row it names and, for an account, a debt or a source of pay, to the one with that name. Sort it, add lines, write numbers any way you like ($1,234.56, 24.99%, 6/3/2026, yes): every line is shown, with what it would do, before anything saves. A blank cell leaves the row as it is; nothing becomes a zero. One undo takes the whole lot back.

The app zip is the source from GitHub. Unzip it and open index.html through a small local server (on a computer: python3 -m http.server in that folder, then localhost:8000). Opened straight from the folder, browsers refuse to read the data files, so the rooms would show without their tables. Your numbers are not in the zip; they stay in this browser and in your backup file.

Replace what is here, or add to it

A file this app downloaded, from this browser or another. Replace makes it the household. Add keeps everything entered here and brings in what the file has that this does not — new accounts, debts, lines, people — without overwriting a number.

Paste in new numbers

A line each, sorted into where it belongs

A statement, a note to yourself, a CSV. Each line with an amount is placed as a debt, an account, a monthly expense, or pay — and you can change any placement before it is taken. One undo takes the whole lot back.
