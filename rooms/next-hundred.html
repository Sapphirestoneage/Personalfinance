Your Next $100

Every place the next hundred dollars could go, on one scale: the return it earns or saves. Guaranteed is guaranteed; expected is a range. They are never added together.

What the numbers say

    How the scale works

    Paying down a debt saves its rate, for certain. Capturing a match brings the employer's cents on the dollar at once. Investing earns whatever the decade brings: the middle of the historical range is shown with its low and high. The order of operations is a note on each line, never a re-sort: the scale is the return, the ladder is the framework.

    Educational tool, not financial advice. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
