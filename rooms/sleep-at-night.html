Your number

What amount would let you stop thinking about it?

Not what a rule says. What you would need sitting in cash to stop doing the arithmetic at 3am. There is no wrong answer here — this is a feeling, stored as a number, and nothing else in SPARKS overwrites it.

Your highest deductible

The first thing this cushion has to cover

Before a cushion is months of expenses, it is one insurance excess — health, car or home, whichever is largest. The FOO ladder's very first step is exactly this, and it reads the number from here rather than asking you for it again.

Coverage checkup

What a bad year can cost, and what stands behind you

Four facts about your cover, asked here once. The Statement's worst plausible year reads the out-of-pocket maximum from this card. Leave blank what you do not have — blank means "not entered", never zero, and the read-out says so.

Yours vs. the maths

The left one is what you said you need. The right one is what your cash currently covers, at your current spending. They are allowed to disagree — that gap is the useful part.
