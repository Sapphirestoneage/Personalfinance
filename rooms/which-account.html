Roth vs. Traditional vs. taxable

Which account wins?

Compared on the same pre-tax cost, which is the only fair way: $7,000 into a Roth costs you more take-home than $7,000 into a Traditional, so comparing equal deposits compares two different decisions. Every box below is a what-if. The ones with a line under them started from your Ledger.

If you plan to stop early, your tax rate in those years is often lower than you'd guess, which tends to favor Traditional now.
