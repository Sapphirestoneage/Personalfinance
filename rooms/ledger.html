This app has a lot of rooms. This is the short route through the ones that matter for you — five sets, in order, with an end.

What this checklist is, and is not

Nothing in this app is locked behind this list. Every room is open from the map at any time, in any order, whether you use this or not. This is a suggested route with somewhere to tick things off.

A step is done when you say it is — not when the room has a number in every box. Those are different things. A room can be full of figures you do not trust yet, and a room can be finished the moment you have decided it does not change anything.

“Not for me” is a real answer. It counts as dealt with, it moves the bar, and it is not held against you anywhere. You can change your mind on any step later.

Clearing the list clears the ticks and nothing else. Every figure you have entered stays exactly where it is.

Not financial advice. Nothing here is sent anywhere; every figure stays in this browser.
