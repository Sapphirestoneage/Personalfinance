What it loses

Depreciation is the largest cost of owning a car and the only one that never sends a bill. The steepest part of it is over before the first service.

    What it costs to run

    Shares of a year of running costs, not dollars — what a car costs to run depends on you, your postcode and your mileage, and this page knows none of those. The order is the point.
