          What one more point is worth

          Spend less, and it counts twice

          Cutting spending raises what you put away and lowers the number you are aiming at, because the target is built from what a year of your life costs. That is why a point of savings rate moves the date so much more than a point of return does. Nothing here is saved — it is a look, not a change.
