The Account You Left Behind

You left a job and the retirement plan stayed there. There are four things you can do with it, one of them is much worse than the others, and there is a piece of paperwork in the middle that costs a fifth of the balance if you get it wrong.

Before anything else

Nothing you type on this page is saved. The balance below is a what-if about an account this app has never seen and cannot see. It is used to work out one figure, it stays in this browser tab, and reloading the page forgets it. No other room will ever read it, and your household record is not touched. The figures that are yours — your age, your income, your filing status, your state — are read here and never written, and each one links to the room that owns it at the bottom of the page.
