First Look

A few questions, one to a screen. Rough numbers are fine — you can correct any of them later, and everything below moves when you do. Nothing leaves this browser.

Where are you right now?

This decides which questions you get. The ones that do not apply to you are not asked at all.

What lands in your account each month?

After tax and everything taken out — the figure your bank shows. A round guess is fine.

$

Gross pay, a year. Take-home is then estimated at three quarters of it and marked rough.

$

What is coming in right now?

Everything that arrives in a month: unemployment, severance spread over the months it covers, side work. Zero is a real answer, and this room handles it.

$

What does housing cost you?

Rent, or mortgage plus what keeps the lights on. One number a month, roughly.

$

Everything else, roughly?

Food, getting around, phone, the rest of it. One number a month. Nobody knows this one exactly.

$

What do you have saved or invested?

One number, everything together. Nearest hundred is close enough.

$

Does your job match retirement contributions?

Not sure is a real answer, and it is the one this room can do something about.

Which finish line are you aiming at?

This sets how much of the pot you would live on a year. Not decided is fine — the cautious rate is assumed, and you can change it on the next screen.
