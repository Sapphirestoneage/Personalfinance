      Before anything else

      There is no score on this page, and there cannot be. A credit score is worked out by a credit bureau — a company that keeps a file on you — from records this app has never seen: every account in your name, every payment, every time someone checked you out. Nothing here adds up to a number out of 850, and any page that offered you one from what you have typed into it would be making it up. What this room does instead is show what goes into a score, say which two parts of it your own figures already answer, and be plain about the three they do not.
