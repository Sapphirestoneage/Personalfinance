How I got here

I spent my early career auditing funds at Deloitte, which is a strange place to learn that money is mostly a feelings problem. The people who were fine weren’t the ones who knew the most. They were the ones who’d stopped being scared of looking. I’ve since coached {{CLIENT_COUNT}} people through that same shift, built a set of free tools so more people can do it without me, and spent time on the BiggerPockets Money podcast and with ChooseFI talking about what I’ve found.
