    Am I in the green everywhere?

    One spoke per number that has a healthy range behind it. Some are the same arithmetic under the name people usually ask for — emergency fund, runway and liquidity all divide the cash you can reach by a month of spending, so they read the same on purpose. Further out is better. The dashed circle is the line you want to be on or outside; a dot inside it is below the usual range. The numbers round the edge match the list below.

      This is a view, not a score. Each spoke stands on its own band, and nothing here adds them together. Adding them up is a different question with a different answer — it needs weights, the weights depend on your age, and every one of them is a judgement call. That lives in The Score, which shows its weighting rather than hiding it. Picking weights here to make a tidier shape would be answering that question by accident.
