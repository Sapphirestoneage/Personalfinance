Terms

The plain terms.

Education, not advice

Everything on this site — the tools, the writing, the frameworks and the coaching — is education. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and Eli Saperstein is not a registered investment advisor. Nothing here recommends any product, security or strategy for you in particular. Decisions about your money are yours; if you need a registered advisor, an accountant or a lawyer for a decision, get one.

The tools

The Money Rooms are provided as they are, free, with no promise that they are complete or correct for your situation. The tax rates, limits and benchmarks they use are approximations with stated caveats, and can be out of date; check any figure against a primary source before relying on it. The source code is public so the formulas can be checked.

Coaching

Coaching is a conversation about your situation and what you might do next. It is not a guarantee of any outcome. The terms of a paid package — what it includes, how it is paid for, and how to cancel — are set out when you book it.
